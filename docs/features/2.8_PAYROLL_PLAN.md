## 1. Summary
Frontend only, inside `frontend-app/`. Add a mock Payroll feature on top of the done F-2.6 Employee master and F-2.7 Leave: a pure payroll calculation (gross = basic + allowance + commission + over time; net = gross - nopay - advance - EPF - loan - tax, never below zero with a warning; EPF/ETF only when the employee flags are on; loan always N/A) driven by placeholder rates and a tax-slab table in mock payroll settings (A9, OQ15 - not payroll law). An Employees Salaries page at `/employees/payroll` with Monthly Pay and Daily Pay tabs, Keyword/Location/Year/Month filters (Filter, Reset, current month first), the 15 columns, N/A cells, display-only Unpaid/Paid badge, paging 10/25/50, Back link, Export CSV and PDF of all filtered rows (reusing the F-2.6 export approach). A row action menu (Admin only) with Add Leave (quick approved one-day Casual Leave via the F-2.7 leave rules), Add Salary Advance (Counter, Date, Amount) and Add Nopay Amount (Date, Amount, Reason) modals; the row recalculates after each. Payroll becomes its own permission-matrix row (`payroll`: Admin Full, Manager View, others None) shown in Users and roles, with no new sidebar item. All data is session-only through the F-2.1 in-memory data services. Design reference (layout and fields only, A8): `docs/design/hr-reference/5.png`, `6.png`, `7.png`, `8.png`.

## 4. Data Model & Migrations
- No database, no migrations. In-memory models only. Money in integer cents (`money.util.ts`, `Rs. 1,234.00`, columns headed `(Rs.)`, right-aligned); dates ISO via `date.util.ts`; rates as numbers; hours/days as numbers.
- Payroll values are never stored: every row is recalculated from employees (basic, allowances, rates, EPF/ETF flags, salary type), approved leave, inputs, advances, nopay and settings. Only inputs, advances, nopay entries and payment records are stored.
- Mock settings carry the placeholder rates (employee EPF 8% of Basic, employer EPF 12%, employer ETF 3%, tax slab table applied to Gross, daily-rate divisor 30) and are labelled "sample values, not payroll law" wherever shown. Employer EPF/ETF are computed but have no column.
- Loan Deduction is always `N/A`. Unpaid leave never auto-creates nopay (OQ16). Payment status is display-only (OQ19).
- Data services return deep copies. Everything resets on refresh. Rollback strategy: not applicable.

## 5. API Contract (draft)
No HTTP API. The contract is the abstract payroll data service plus `PayrollService`, `PayrollAdminService`, `PayrollExportService`. Routes:

| Route | Purpose | Access |
|---|---|---|
| `/employees/payroll` | Salaries page, tabs, export | Employees view AND Payroll view (Admin Full, Manager View; others none) |
| Row menu actions (Add Leave / Salary Advance / Nopay) | modals on the page | Payroll Full (Admin); hidden for View |

The session permission matrix decides (editable in Users and roles; AC-70). Manager cannot add anything; Export is a read action available to View users.

## 6. Threat Model (STRIDE-lite — MANDATORY)
Context: no backend, fictional data, client-side gating is UX only (stated in the story). Controls must be correct so a real backend can enforce the same rules later. Payroll is the most sensitive data in the app (pay, tax, deductions).

| STRIDE | Applies? | Risk in this feature | Mitigation / control |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S**poofing (identity) | Y | Adding advances, nopay or leave as someone else; quick-leave approver identity forged; counter chosen to hide who recorded it. | Actor read from the session inside `PayrollAdminService`, never from input. Quick-leave approver is derived by the service from the signed-in user (or an eligible fallback), never from the form. Ids, `createdAt` and statuses are service-set. Counter is informational only and validated against the settings counter list. |
| **T**ampering (integrity) | Y | Crafted dates/amounts (NaN, negative, scientific notation, huge, 31 Feb), advance above that month's gross by stale form or double submit, tampering with the derived net, tax-slab misuse, rounding drift, shared-reference mutation of seeds, quick leave bypassing balance/overlap rules, editing Paid history. | All parsing/bounds in `payroll-validation.util.ts`; gross recomputed from fresh data inside the service at call time; in-flight guard; calculations in one pure util with band-edge and rounding specs (integer cents, half-up); only inputs/advances/nopay are stored, never derived values; leave goes through the F-2.7 validation (balance, overlap, leave year); Paid status has no write path in this phase; deep copies from data services. |
| **R**epudiation | Y (low) | No audit trail of who added an advance, nopay entry or leave. | Entries store date, amount, reason, counter and `createdAt`; quick leave stores approver and comment "Added from Payroll". Actor is not otherwise logged in a session-only frontend. Documented limitation. |
| **I**nformation Disclosure | Y | Salaries, allowances, tax and deductions seen by roles without Payroll access; pay data in exports/toasts/URLs; Manager seeing more than View; export leaking beyond the filtered rows. | Own `payroll` permission row; `moduleAccessActivateGuard` on `/employees/payroll` with `data.module = PAYROLL` (Pharmacist/Cashier blocked by URL, AC-67); row menu and Add actions hidden without Full; export includes only the filtered rows and the documented columns; toasts name the employee and outcome only, never amounts, reasons or comments; no values placed in query params or logged; no `[innerHTML]`, no `bypassSecurityTrust*`, no `console.*`, nothing in `localStorage` / `sessionStorage`; Blob object URLs revoked. |
| **D**enial of Service | Y (low) | Huge reason text, very large paging/exports, rapid clicks, repeated recalculation. | Max lengths (reason 250); list paginated 10/25/50; export built only from in-memory filtered rows; calculation O(rows) with `computed` memoisation; in-flight guard; `takeUntilDestroyed` on subscriptions. |
| **E**levation of Privilege | Y | Manager (View) adding advance/nopay/leave through the row menu, direct handler calls, or a changed matrix; Employees Full without Payroll access reaching payroll; CSV formula injection reaching a recipient's spreadsheet; Admin removing own Payroll access mid-session. | `canWrite(PAYROLL)` re-checked inside every `PayrollAdminService` method; menu hidden with `*appCanWrite`-style check on the PAYROLL key; route guard on PAYROLL; permissions read from the live matrix (AC-70); `normaliseMatrix` keeps Admin FULL for every module including PAYROLL; CSV cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab or CR neutralised (reuse). |

### 6.1 Assets & Trust Boundaries
- Sensitive data: employee salaries, allowances, commission, tax, EPF/ETF figures, advances, nopay reasons. All fictional.
- Trust boundaries: URL (route/query params), every modal input and filter are untrusted; the exported file leaves the app to the user's device (CSV injection boundary). No browser storage used.

### 6.2 Pre-emptive OWASP mapping
- Likely-in-play: A01 (route and action gating, own matrix row, Manager view-only), A03 (CSV formula injection, XSS via reasons/names rendered only by interpolation), A04 (insecure design: client-only gating documented, advance-vs-gross stale submit, derived values never stored), A05 (no debug output), A08 (service-set fields, calculation integrity), A09 (limited audit, documented). A02 does not apply to mock rates but the "not payroll law" labelling is a correctness/disclosure requirement. Reviewer still does a full A01 to A10 pass.

## 9. Risks & Trade-offs
- R1 **Calculation correctness vs placeholder law:** rates and tax slabs are mock (A9, OQ15). Mitigation: all in settings seed, labelled, unit-tested at band edges; rounding rule documented in the util header and spec.
- R2 **Permission matrix gains a key:** adding `ModuleKey.PAYROLL` touches `module-definitions`, matrix order, seeds, role specs, sidebar and Users and roles. Risk of regressions in the F-2.4 specs and of the sidebar listing a hidden module. Mitigation: `hideFromSidebar` flag, `moduleKeyFromUrl` untouched for `/employees/...`, normalise defaulting for missing key, run the full suite at the batch 2 checkpoint.
- R3 **Quick leave semantic:** F-2.7 `apply` creates Pending; payroll must create an Approved leave. A small, rule-preserving extension of `LeaveAdminService` is required (approver derived by the service). Risk: weakening "nobody approves their own request". Mitigation: approver must be eligible and never the applicant; fallback to another eligible Admin/Manager; reviewer checks this specifically.
- R4 **Month assignment:** advances, nopay and leave belong to the month of their date; leave spanning months is one-day only here so no split.
- R5 **Derived values not stored:** keeps data consistent, but recalculating on every filter change must stay cheap (`computed`, paging before heavy formatting).
- R6 **Large workstream (weights 8+5+5+3+3):** mitigate by three sequential batches with checkpoints (section 11).
- R7 **Client-only gating** documented limitation in the story.

## 10. Out of Scope
- Real payroll law or verified rates; loans; unpaid-leave automation (OQ16); Mark as paid, payslips, bank files (OQ19); on-screen entry of overtime hours (OQ13); editing payroll settings from a screen; employer EPF/ETF columns; real counters; persistence beyond the session; sidebar item for Payroll; changes to F-2.6 / F-2.7 behaviour other than the quick-leave reuse point; exact colours (OQ1), numeric performance targets (OQ6), smallest width (OQ5); other languages.
