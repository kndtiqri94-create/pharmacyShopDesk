## 1. Summary
Frontend only, inside `frontend-app/`, session-only mock data, built on the Phase A till (`/pos`, `PosService`, `PosSaleService`, `SalesDataService`). Adds: (1) a second bill type "Return" (original invoice lookup by number, earlier return lookup, partial line returns capped at sold-minus-returned, stock restored to the original batch else the earliest-expiry batch, cash refund or named-customer credit, return record with own number, printable return receipt); (2) quotations (Save Quotation F11, Quotations list, open, convert to Invoice, delete; no stock or sales effect until converted); (3) Last Invoice view and reprint; (4) Cash Book, Add OB (opening balance) and Drawer (End); (5) Counter Close with locked day report; (6) Due Amount (customer balances from Credit sales, take payment), Update Products (selling price and stock quick edit, with confirmation) and Bill & Reload link. A bottom bar of the nine Phase B buttons appears on the till for users with POS access only. A new cash ledger service derives the day's cash figures from sales, returns and due payments; Reload & Utility is never included. No Transfer, no "Normal" button, no customer add, no hardware.

## 4. Data Model & Migrations
- No database, no migrations. In-memory, session only; resets on refresh. Rollback: not applicable.
- Money in integer cents (`money.util.ts`, `Rs. 1,234.00`, right-aligned, two decimals). Dates via `date.util.ts` and `ClockService`. All ids, numbers, timestamps and actors are service-set.
- Returns never edit or delete the original sale; they are separate records linked by `originalSaleId`. Returnable quantity is computed (sold − sum of earlier returns for that sale line), never stored as a mutable counter.
- Cash ledger entries are DERIVED from sales (cash received − change), returns (cash refunds), due payments (cash method) and the day opening; not stored independently, so they cannot drift. Reload & Utility transactions are never read.
- Customer balance = Credit sale amounts − due payments − return credits, floored at zero in the view.
- Counter close writes one locked report per date; after that opening, entries and report for that date are immutable at the data-service level (AC-57). Later sales on a closed day are allowed but excluded from the locked report (AC-59).
- Update Products writes through the existing product data path plus an adjustment audit record (old/new, by, at).

## 6. Threat Model (STRIDE-lite — MANDATORY)
Context: no backend, fictional data; client-side gating is UX only (stated in the story). Controls are still written as a real backend would enforce them. Sensitive items: cash figures and the counter report (trusted, locked), refund and credit amounts, customer balances, price and stock edits.

| STRIDE | Applies? | Risk in this feature | Mitigation / control |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S**poofing (identity) | Y | A return, due payment, opening balance, counter close or product edit attributed to the wrong user; actor supplied by the form. | Actor (`processedBy`, `takenBy`, `closedBy`, `by`) is always read from the signed-in session inside the service, never from dialog input; ids, numbers and timestamps are service-set; every write re-checks the live session. |
| **T**ampering (integrity) | Y | Return quantity above returnable (crafted NaN, negative, huge, decimals), return of an invoice twice, refund amount edited client-side, due payment above owed, product price/stock edited to NaN or negative, locked day modified through a direct service/data call, stock drift between return/product update and record write, double submit, quotation converted twice or priced stale, opening balance silently replaced. | One validation util per flow used by both UI and services; refund/credit and totals are recomputed from the original sale line price and validated quantity at save, never taken from the UI; returnable quantity recomputed from fresh sales+returns; owed re-read fresh before payment; integer cents with strict numeric parsing (reject NaN, negatives, 3+ decimals, non-integer stock); the in-memory data service itself rejects writes to a closed day (opening, entries, report) so the lock is not UI-only; stock restore and return record written in one synchronous block with copies first; busy/in-flight guard on every save; quotation status transition OPEN to CONVERTED enforced in the service and only after the invoice is saved; opening balance change requires confirmation and writes a change-log entry; deep copies returned from data services. |
| **R**epudiation | Y | A refund, credit, due payment, opening balance change, price/stock edit or counter close cannot be traced. | Each record stores actor name/id and time; opening balance changes keep old/new/by/at; product adjustments keep old/new/by/at; counter report stores who closed and when; closed report is immutable. Session-only limitation documented. |
| **I**nformation Disclosure | Y | Cash figures, counter reports, customer balances or sales shown to users without POS access; toasts or receipts leaking amounts; customer names in logs. | Bottom bar and all Phase B dialogs only render with `canView(POS)`; `/pos` route guards unchanged; no `console.*`; nothing in `localStorage`/`sessionStorage`/query params/route state; toasts show numbers (invoice, return, quotation, receipt) not amounts; no `[innerHTML]`, no `bypassSecurityTrust*`; receipts rendered by template binding and printed with `window.print()` (no `document.write`); no card or bank detail captured (only method and optional reference, length capped). |
| **D**enial of Service | Y (low) | Huge lookup/search strings, long note text, very large return line counts, unbounded quotation/due lists, F11 repeat, listener leaks. | Length caps on invoice/return lookup, search, note and reference inputs; line count and quantity bounds (reuse `MAX_BILL_LINES`); list rendering capped/paged by the existing DataTable; in-flight guards; F11 and other key handlers registered only while the till is alive and removed on destroy; `takeUntilDestroyed`. |
| **E**levation of Privilege | Y | A View-only or no-POS user performs a return, closes the counter, takes a payment or edits stock; a user reopens a closed counter or edits a locked report (including Admin); reaches Reload & Utility through Bill & Reload without access. | `canWrite(POS)` re-checked in every Phase B service method (not only buttons hidden); buttons hidden without `canView(POS)`; the lock has no override for any role (AC-57); Bill & Reload checks `canView(RELOAD_UTILITY)` and shows a message instead of navigating; permissions read from the live matrix. |

## 9. Risks & Trade-offs
- R1: Session-only data means cash book, dues and locked reports vanish on refresh. Mitigation: documented in the story; empty states handled.
- R2: F11 is browser-owned (fullscreen). Mitigation: `preventDefault` on the till only, button shows its key, Phase A fullscreen toggle stays the supported route; if the browser refuses to suppress F11 the button still works.
- R3: Stock changes from returns and Update Products are deliberate exceptions to "only a GRN changes stock" (like A11); both must be atomic with their record and audited. Update Products stock edit (B-3) is the riskiest; isolated in one service so it is cheap to remove if the Product Owner later reverses B-3.
- R4: Dues from Credit sales need a consistent balance source. Mitigation: derive from sales, due payments and return credits in one util; no stored balance field to drift.
- R5: Returned goods affect Today's sales and profit; profit effect of returns (restock cost) is computed from the restock allocation cost to avoid overstating profit. Hasika documents the rounding in the util.
- R6: Phase B starts only after Phase A acceptance; Phase A files are modified, so regressions in `pos.component.spec.ts` are likely and must be updated.
- R7: Dialog-based screens keep the unsaved bill intact by never navigating; Bill & Reload is the only navigation and asks first.

## 10. Out of Scope
- Transfer bills, "Normal" view button, Retail/Wholesale, inline customer add, Reload and Utility in POS cash/returns/dues, real hardware or backend, credit limits/statements/interest, rep commission, returns without an original invoice, supplier returns, editing or deleting saved sales, reopening a closed counter, cost/batch/expiry editing in Update Products, multiple tills.

## 12.2 Data Model & Migrations
No database or migrations; in-memory, session only. Money in integer cents. Report remains one locked record per date. Handover does not enter Expected Cash, difference or outcome (AC-93): expected = opening + POS cash in - POS cash out, same as AC-52/AC-80. BF default = counted - handover (A23), becomes the next business day's opening pre-fill, stored on the report only (no separate store). Start Time = earliest of first POS sale time and opening `setAt`; if neither exists the screen shows a clear "No activity yet" indication (not an error). Total Sales and Sales Breakdown are POS-only; Reload & Utility never read.

## 12.3 API Contract
No HTTP API. Contract is `PosCounterService.preview()` / `close(input)`, `PosCashService` helpers, and the extended `CounterReport`. No new route; the dialog opens from the till as today. Access unchanged: `canView(POS)` to see, `canWrite(POS)` re-checked in `close`.

## 12.4 Threat Model (STRIDE-lite, S-2.10.7 delta) — MANDATORY
Context unchanged (no backend; fictional data; client gating is UX only, controls written as a real backend would enforce). New sensitive data: handover amount, supervisor identity, BF amount, unaccounted remainder, shift times.

## 12.5 Test Strategy
- Utils at band edges: handover 0 / equal to counted / counted + 0.01 / negative / NaN / 3 decimals; BF at bound and one cent over; unaccounted zero and positive; canComplete states; denomination totals incl. zero counts, fractional/negative counts refused, large cap.
- Service: denied role, View-only role, no supervisor when handover > 0, supervisor not Admin/Manager, inactive supervisor, stale expected, handover does not change difference or outcome, BF default and bound, already closed day refused, end time set at close, Reload & Utility excluded from Total Sales and expected, Start Time from first sale or opening.
- Components: three-column and stacked layout classes, bilingual strings exactly AC-74 and English-only for AC-75 labels, no Reload Commission anywhere, Complete disabled with hint then enabled then disabled again, reconciliation empty then populated with word and icon, calculator opens dialog, confirm fills field, cancel leaves it, focus return, Escape, locked view without Complete, unsaved-bill warning (AC-98), 52px controls, Add OB pre-fill from previous BF with source text.
- Negative tests: unauthenticated, no POS access, View-only, NaN/negative/huge/extra decimals, oversized input, stale data, locked day.
- Per project rule, agents do not auto-run the suite; the user runs `npm test` and the build.
