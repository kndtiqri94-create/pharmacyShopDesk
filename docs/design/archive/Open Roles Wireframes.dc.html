1Open roles — anatomy first, then page structure

Grey bars are text, dashed boxes are inputs, dark bar is the primary action. No brand colour or type yet. Notes in the margin are behaviour, not styling.

1aRole card anatomy — every element it has to carry
A · JOB TITLE
E · OPEN ROLE
B · LOCATION · WORK TYPE · SENIORITY
C · ONE-LINE SUMMARY
D · SKILL TAGS
A — one line, truncates with ellipsis at 360px. The whole card is the link to the role page.
B — plain text, middle dots between. Not chips, so they don't compete with the skill tags.
C — max 2 lines, clamped. Pulled from the first sentence of the role description.
D — up to 4 tags, then "+3". Tags are display only here; filtering comes later if you want it.
E — Apply goes straight to the form on the role page. On mobile it drops below the tags, full width.

Two ways in on purpose: the card body opens the role, the button skips to applying. Open question at the bottom of 1b about whether posted date and salary belong here too.

1bPage structure — desktop 1040px
1 · PAGE HEADING
2 · search filters the list live as you type. Count of matching roles sits to the right.
3 · ROLE LIST — CARD FROM 1a, STACKED
16px between cards. No pagination until there are more than ~20 roles.
4 · empty state when search matches nothing — clear search, or register interest

Single column, full width, no side filters. Worth deciding: does the card also need posted date and salary, and should the list group by team once there are more roles?
