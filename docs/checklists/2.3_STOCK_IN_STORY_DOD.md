## Deviations and flags for the reviewer
1. **GRN numbering vs seed.** The seed held GRN-0208..0212, so the next derived number would have been GRN-0213, while the story and design say GRN-0212. Seed renumbered (cancelled GRN now GRN-0207, draft GRN now GRN-0211) so the next GRN is GRN-0212 and the next PO is PO-0150. Seed amounts, dates and statuses are unchanged; no other spec referenced the numbers.
2. **Date range filters.** "This month" (GRN) and "Last 30 days" (PO) filter non-draft records by received or ordered date; Draft GRNs and Draft POs always stay visible so a saved draft never disappears because its date is outside the range. The range buttons are display-only (a sample-only message appears on press).
3. **PO prefill quantity.** A GRN started from a PO fills the quantity still outstanding (ordered less already received), and shows "ordered N" with the full ordered quantity. For a fresh PO this equals the ordered quantity (AC-22).
4. **"Expected within 7 days" tile note** counts open POs due on or before 7 days from today, including overdue ones. Wording: "Expected within 7 days: N".
5. **Batch-tracked product with opening stock but no batches.** Receiving adds a batch and stock then comes from batches only (existing F-2.2 `getStockOnHand` rule), so any unbatched opening stock is no longer counted. No seed product is in this state.
6. **Tests not executed** (see DoD.19).
