## S-2.10.7 deliberate decisions to flag
- The reference screenshot uses tinted gradient cards. `docs/design/CLAUDE.md` says no gradients, glass or tinted cards, so the three cards use the plain surface, hairline border and token colours of the ShopDesk design system. The only tinted element is the Expected Cash banner, using the status token `--sd-info-soft` (both themes). The reference image was not copied into the repo.
- Counted cash must be valid for the Complete button to enable (AC-82 to 84). A handover without a supervisor, or an invalid handover or BF, does not disable the button; pressing it shows the message (AC-89 to 91). The service re-validates everything.
- Total Sales is the sum of POS invoice totals for the day (not netted for returns), matching "POS sales only" in AC-78.
- BF text left blank is treated as 0 (an explicit entry); the service default (counted less handover) applies only when no BF text is sent.
- `broughtForwardFor` finds the previous closed day by looking back up to 31 days, one date at a time, because no list-all-reports method exists and the plan forbids new data service methods.
