## 1. Story
As a **pharmacy owner (Admin) or manager**, I want **to see each employee's pay for a chosen month (basic salary, allowances, commission, overtime, advances, nopay, EPF and income tax, gross and net), record a one-day leave, a salary advance or a nopay amount from the pay row, and export the list** so that **the shop knows what each person earns and is owed, deductions are applied consistently, and staff pay can be reviewed and shared without a spreadsheet**.

## 2. Business Context
After the Employee master (F-2.6) and Leave management (F-2.7), Payroll is the last step of the HR sequence: it reads each employee's stored salary, allowances, EPF and ETF choices, rates and approved leave, and turns them into a monthly figure. It serves PRD goals G1 and G3. The reference images (`docs/design/hr-reference/5.png` Salaries, `6.png` row actions, `7.png` Create Leave, `8.png` Create Salary Advance) are used for layout and field content only; the ShopDesk look and feel governs styling (A8). All figures are sample data for the current session; the statutory rates and income-tax bands are placeholders, not verified payroll law (A9).

## 3. Scope
### 3.1 In Scope
- **S-2.8.1 Pay calculation (sample data).** For each employee and month:
  - Gross Salary = Basic Salary + Allowance + Commission + Over Time.
  - Over Time = overtime hours for the month x the employee's Over Time Rate.
  - Commission = the employee's stored Commission Rate (%) applied to a sample commission base for that employee and month (not linked to real sales).
  - Deductions = Total Nopay + Salary Advance + EPF Deductions + Loan Deduction + Payee Tax.
  - Net Salary = Gross Salary minus Deductions, never below zero; if deductions exceed gross, a warning with words is shown on that row.
  - EPF Deductions apply only when the employee has Calculate EPF on; employer EPF and ETF cost apply only when Calculate EPF / Calculate ETF are on, and are employer cost, not deducted from the employee.
  - Payee Tax is worked out from a monthly income-tax slab table.
  - Total Nopay = the sum of that month's Add Nopay Amount entries.
  - Loan Deduction shows N/A (loans are out of scope).
  - Rates and tax bands are placeholders held in adjustable sample settings, not fixed in screens.
  - The figures recalculate whenever salary, allowances, leave, advances or nopay change.
  - For employees whose Salary Type is Daily, Gross uses Daily Rate x days worked (sample days worked) in place of Basic Salary.
- **S-2.8.2 Employees Salaries page.** Back link; tabs Monthly Pay and Daily Pay; filters Keyword, Location, Year, Month with Filter and Reset; table columns Name (with EMP code or mobile beneath), Basic Salary, Allowance, Commission, Salary Advance, Leaves, Total Nopay, EPF Deductions, Loan Deduction, Payee Tax, Over Time, Gross Salary, Net Salary, Payment Status, Action; money columns headed (Rs.), right-aligned, two decimals; N/A where a value does not apply; Payment Status shown as the word Unpaid or Paid; page size and "Showing a to b of N entries" with pagination. Sample employees have salaries so the table is not all zero.
- **S-2.8.3 Row actions.** A row action menu with Add Leave, Add Salary Advance and Add Nopay Amount, each opening a small form; the pay row recalculates straight after each.
  - Add Leave (quick leave): Date only; creates an approved one-day leave using the existing leave rules (balance, overlap, leave year).
  - Add Salary Advance: Counter, Date, Advance Amount.
  - Add Nopay Amount: Date, Amount, Reason.
  - The Salary Advance and Leaves cells open the underlying records.
- **S-2.8.4 Export.** Export CSV and Export PDF of the currently filtered pay rows, every column, produced in the browser.
- **S-2.8.5 Routes, tabs and access.** Payroll is reached from the Employees area (a Payroll tab or link beside Staff, Attendance and Leave, with the Back link returning to Employees); no new item in the main sidebar. Access: Admin Full, Manager View only, Pharmacist and Cashier none; the Users and roles permission matrix shows Payroll as its own row under Employees.
- **Decisions by the user (confirmed 2026-10-17):** HR scope is delivered one feature at a time; this story covers Payroll only. All data is sample data kept only for the current session; frontend-only; commission and discounts are stored fields not wired into sales.

### 3.3 Other accepted assumptions (none changes a confirmed requirement)
- A1: placement under Employees, no new sidebar item. A6: Daily Pay inferred (see OQ12). A7: Counter is a mock till (see OQ17). A8: references give layout and fields only; styling follows ShopDesk; dates like `21 Sep 2026`. A9: statutory rates are placeholders (see OQ15).
- Year and Month start on the current month by the session date; Year choices are the previous, current and next year; Location choices are the employees' stored locations; the Keyword matches name, EMP code or mobile, ignoring capital letters.
- The page lists employees who are not Inactive. An employee with no salary entered still appears, with zero figures.
- Money shows as `Rs.` amounts with thousands separators and two decimals, in the shop's usual money format.
- A salary advance, nopay amount or leave belongs to the month of the date chosen; the pay row for that month is the one that changes.
- Access follows the permissions in force in the session (Admin can change them in Users and roles).
- Payroll changes (advances, nopay, quick leave) are kept for the current session only. Quick leave also appears in the Leave tab, Leave Balance and On leave counts because it is a real leave record in the session.

### 3.4 Out of Scope (explicit)
- Marking a row as Paid, payslips, payment runs, bank files, and any real payment.
- Loans (Loan Deduction is always N/A), bonuses, arrears, leave encashment, unpaid leave types, half-day leave, public-holiday rules.
- Editing overtime hours, days worked or commission base on screen; linking commission to real sales; any attendance or time-clock feature.
- Verified statutory rates and tax law; employer EPF and ETF reporting or filing.
- Editing or deleting a recorded advance, nopay entry or quick leave from the Payroll page (a quick leave is managed as normal leave in the Leave tab).
- Changing F-2.6 Employee master or F-2.7 Leave management behaviour; real file storage; keeping any payroll data beyond the current session.
- Exact colour values (OQ1), numeric performance targets (OQ6), the smallest supported width (OQ5); languages other than English.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)
Cross-cutting rules for every screen below (not repeated per criterion): works in light and dark themes, reflows on smaller screens, follows the ShopDesk look and content rules (plain language, `21 Sep 2026`, no emoji or exclamation marks), is operable by keyboard with visible focus, status always shown as a word, only one primary button per screen, error messages say what to fix, and respects role access.

### 4.1 Pay calculation (S-2.8.1)
- **AC-1** — Given an employee and a month, when pay is worked out, then Gross Salary equals Basic Salary plus Allowance plus Commission plus Over Time, using the Basic Salary and allowances stored on the employee; for a Daily employee, the Basic Salary part is Daily Rate times Days Worked.
- **AC-2** — Given an employee and a month, then Net Salary equals Gross Salary minus Total Nopay, Salary Advance, EPF Deductions, Loan Deduction and Payee Tax.
- **AC-3** — Given an employee with overtime hours in the sample data for the month, then Over Time equals those hours times the employee's Over Time Rate; given no hours for that month, then Over Time is zero.
- **AC-4** — Given an employee whose Over Time Rate is empty or zero, then Over Time is zero and no error is shown.
- **AC-5** — Given an employee with a stored Commission Rate and a sample commission base for the month, then Commission equals the rate percent of that base; given no rate or no base, then Commission is zero.
- **AC-6** — Given an employee with Calculate EPF on, then EPF Deductions equal the placeholder employee EPF rate times Basic Salary; given it is off, then EPF Deductions are zero.
- **AC-7** — Given an employee with Calculate EPF or Calculate ETF on, then the employer EPF and ETF costs are calculated at the placeholder employer rates and are not deducted from Net Salary; given the flags are off, then they are zero.
- **AC-8** — Given a Gross Salary, then Payee Tax equals the amount given by the placeholder monthly income-tax slab table for that gross; given a gross at or below the first band, then Payee Tax is zero.
- **AC-9** — Given the placeholder rates and slab table, when I look for them, then they are held in adjustable sample settings (not fixed on screens) and any place that shows or explains them says they are sample values, not verified payroll law.
- **AC-10** — Given a rate or slab value in the sample settings is changed, when pay is next worked out, then the results follow the changed value.
- **AC-11** — Given an employee with Add Nopay Amount entries in the month, then Total Nopay equals their sum; given none, then Total Nopay is zero.
- **AC-12** — Given approved leave in the month, then it does not by itself create any nopay or reduce pay.
- **AC-13** — Given Loan Deduction, then it always shows N/A and counts as zero in deductions.
- **AC-14** — Given deductions are larger than Gross Salary, then Net Salary is zero, never negative, and the row shows a warning in words that deductions exceed pay.
- **AC-15** — Given any change to an employee's salary, allowances, Calculate EPF or ETF flags, Over Time Rate or Commission Rate (made in the Employee master), or to leave, advances or nopay, when I next look at the pay row, then it shows the recalculated figures.
- **AC-16** — Given any money result, then it is rounded to two decimals and the components shown on the row add up to the Gross and Net shown.
- **AC-17** — Given the sample data, then the employees have salaries so that the table is not all zero, including at least one employee each with: Calculate EPF on, Calculate ETF on, a Commission Rate, an Over Time Rate, allowances, a Daily Salary Type, a gross high enough to attract Payee Tax, and one with no salary at all.

### 4.3 Row actions (S-2.8.3)
- **AC-35** — Given I am an Admin and choose the action menu on a row, then I see Add Leave, Add Salary Advance and Add Nopay Amount, and it can be opened and used by keyboard.
- **AC-36** — Given I choose Add Leave, when the form opens, then it shows the employee's name, a required Date starting as today, a Save (the one primary button) and Cancel, and it can be dismissed without any change.
- **AC-37** — Given I save Add Leave with a valid Date, then an Approved one-day Casual Leave is created for that employee on that date, a short confirmation is shown, and the form closes.
- **AC-38** — Given the quick leave is created, then it uses up one day of the employee's Casual Leave balance as Utilized (not Pending Approval), appears in the Leave tab as Approved, and counts in On leave if the date is today.
- **AC-39** — Given the date is in the month shown, when the leave is created, then the row's Leaves cell shows the count of leave days and links to the leave records, and the row is recalculated.
- **AC-40** — Given the employee has no Casual Leave balance left, when I save, then it is refused with a message saying how many days are left, and nothing is created.
- **AC-41** — Given the date overlaps a Pending or Approved leave of that employee, or falls outside the leave year, when I save, then it is refused with the same kind of message as on the leave application page and nothing is created.
- **AC-42** — Given the Date is empty, when I save, then nothing is saved and an error says what to fix.
- **AC-43** — Given I choose Add Salary Advance, when the form opens, then it shows Counter, Date (starting as today) and Advance Amount, all required, with Save and Cancel.
- **AC-44** — Given the Counter choice, then it lists the sample cash tills (including Counter1) and choosing one has no effect on any till, float or sales figure.
- **AC-45** — Given an Advance Amount of 0 or less, empty, or not a number, when I save, then it is refused with a message saying the amount must be above 0.
- **AC-46** — Given an Advance Amount greater than the employee's Gross Salary for the month of the chosen Date, when I save, then it is refused with a message saying the advance cannot be more than that month's gross salary and showing that gross.
- **AC-47** — Given a valid advance, when I save, then a confirmation is shown, the advance is added to that month's Salary Advance, Net Salary is recalculated, and the form closes.
- **AC-48** — Given Counter is not chosen, when I save, then nothing is saved and an error says what to fix.
- **AC-49** — Given I choose Add Nopay Amount, when the form opens, then it shows Date (starting as today), Amount and Reason, all required, with Save and Cancel.
- **AC-50** — Given a nopay Amount of 0 or less, empty or not a number, when I save, then it is refused with a message saying the amount must be above 0.
- **AC-51** — Given a nopay Amount greater than the Gross Salary of the chosen month, when I save, then it is refused with a message saying it cannot be more than that month's gross salary.
- **AC-52** — Given a Reason longer than 250 characters or empty, when I save, then it is refused with a message stating the limit or that it is required.
- **AC-53** — Given a valid nopay entry, when I save, then it is added to that month's Total Nopay, Net Salary is recalculated, and a confirmation is shown.
- **AC-54** — Given the Salary Advance cell or the Leaves cell, when I open it, then I see that employee's advances (date, counter, amount) or leave records for the month, read-only; and given a save on any form changed data for a month other than the one shown, then the confirmation names that month.

## 5. Security & Privacy Notes (business-level only)
- Sensitive data involved: employees' salaries, allowances, commission, tax and EPF deductions, advances, nopay entries and reasons, and payment status. All sample data is fictional.
- Who should/shouldn't be able to see or change this: only signed-in people see anything. Admin may see and change everything on Payroll. Manager may see pay information and export it but must not add leave, advances or nopay or change anything. Pharmacist and Cashier must not see payroll information. A person without the required access must not reach the Payroll page or perform a payroll action by typing its address. The placeholder statutory rates must never be presented as correct payroll law. Exported files contain sensitive pay data and are only made on the user's own request.
