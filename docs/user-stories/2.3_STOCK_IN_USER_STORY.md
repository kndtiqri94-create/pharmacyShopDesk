## 1. Story
As a **pharmacy owner, manager or pharmacist**, I want **to raise purchase orders to suppliers, record each delivery I receive against a goods received note (with batches, expiry dates, costs and payment), and see every delivery and order in one place** so that **stock, batch expiry dates and cost prices are always right after a delivery, I know what is still on its way and what I owe suppliers, and I am warned before accepting short-dated stock**.

## 2. Business Context
Stock-in is how medicines enter the shop (use case UC1: receive a delivery). Only a goods received note (GRN) changes stock; a purchase order (PO) only records what was asked for. This feature gives Products real batches and expiry dates (which F-2.2 deliberately left blank), lets the Dashboard's "New GRN", "Create PO" and "Open" actions land on real screens, and supplies the invoice and payment data that Suppliers (F-2.4) will use to settle what is owed. It serves PRD goals G1 and G3 and builds on F-2.1 (look and feel, building blocks, sample data, main frame, role access) and F-2.2 (product and batch data). Colour values remain approximate until exact ones are supplied (PRD OQ1).

## 3. Scope
### 3.1 In Scope
- **S-2.3.1 GRN list.**
  - Heading "Goods received notes" with the subtitle "Every delivery you receive, with batches, expiry and what you owe." and the buttons Export and a primary New GRN.
  - Three summary tiles: Received this month, Waiting to be received, Unpaid to suppliers.
  - Search by GRN number, invoice number or supplier; status chips All / Draft / Received / Cancelled with live counts; a "This month" date button.
  - Table with GRN number, received date, supplier (with "Received by"), invoice and PO reference (or "No PO"), items, total (Rs.), payment badge, status badge and row actions.
- **S-2.3.2 New GRN form.**
  - Breadcrumb, heading "New GRN", a subtitle showing the GRN number and "Draft saved automatically", Save draft and a primary Receive stock.
  - Delivery details: Supplier (required), Purchase order, Received date, Supplier invoice no (required), Invoice date, Received by.
  - Payment: Method, Paid now, Due date.
  - Items received: Scan item and Add item; a table of product, batch no, expiry (MM/YYYY), received, free, cost, price, total and a remove action per line.
  - Totals: number of lines and units, Discount, Total and Balance due after payment.
  - The draft is kept automatically for the session.
  - Expiry-under-six-months warning, and an information note that receiving adds batches and updates cost prices.
- **S-2.3.3 GRN from a PO and receiving effects.**
  - Choosing a PO fills the supplier and lines from it; a GRN may also be started with no PO.
  - Receive stock adds a batch for each line, increases stock, updates cost prices and closes or part-closes the linked PO.
- **S-2.3.4 Purchase order list.**
  - Heading "Purchase orders" with the subtitle "Orders you have sent to suppliers and what is still on the way." and the buttons Export and a primary New purchase order.
  - Search by PO number or supplier; status chips All / Draft / Sent / Part received / Received / Cancelled with live counts; a "Last 30 days" date button.
  - Table with PO number, ordered date, supplier (with contact or an items summary), expected date (overdue shown in danger colour as "20 Sep · overdue"), items, total (Rs.), status and row actions: a soft "Receive" on Sent and Part received orders, edit on Draft, view otherwise, and more.
- **S-2.3.5 New purchase order.**
  - Breadcrumb, heading "New purchase order", a subtitle showing the PO number and "Draft", Save draft, Print and a primary Mark as sent.
  - Order details: Supplier (required), Order date, Expected delivery, Notes to supplier.
  - A side card about the chosen supplier: supplier since, phone, payment terms, usual delivery time and what you owe them.
  - Items to order: Add item and "Add all low-stock items"; table of product, in stock, reorder at, order qty, unit cost, total and a remove action.
  - Totals: lines, units and expected date, Sub-total, Delivery and Total.
- Accepted assumptions (consistent with F-2.1, F-2.2 and PRD open questions; none changes a confirmed requirement):
  - OQ4: Export (both lists), Print and the Scan item button are shown but do nothing real and give a clear "sample only" message. Marking a PO as sent does not send anything to a supplier.
  - All data lives only for the current session, as in F-2.1 and F-2.2; refreshing returns to the original sample set.
  - The "Waiting to be received" tile counts POs that are Sent or Part received; its note counts those expected within 7 days. "Unpaid to suppliers" adds up the balance still owed on received GRNs.
  - A GRN line's total is the received quantity multiplied by its cost; free units add to stock but add nothing to the amount. Total is the sum of line totals less the Discount (an amount in rupees); Balance due after payment is Total less Paid now.
  - Payment status on a GRN is derived: Paid when nothing is left to pay, Part paid when something has been paid and something remains, Unpaid when nothing has been paid.
  - Receiving updates cost prices only (as FR-GRN-5 states); the "Price" figure on a GRN line is the shelf selling price shown for reference and does not change the product's selling price.
  - A PO becomes Received when everything ordered has been received across its GRNs, otherwise Part received. Only a Sent or Part received PO can be received.
  - Row actions on the GRN list are view and more ("more" gives a "sample only" message); a received GRN is not editable. Draft GRNs reopen in the New GRN form. No cancel action is offered; the Cancelled status appears only in the sample data.
  - The expiry warning window is six months, taken as a fixed rule, as the design states it.
  - The Pharmacist (Full on GRN, View on PO) can use "Receive" on a PO because it opens a GRN, but cannot create or edit POs.
  - GRN and PO numbers continue from the sample data (for example the next GRN is GRN-0212 and the next PO is PO-0150).
- With F-2.3 delivered, the Dashboard's "New GRN", "Create PO" and "Open" actions open these real screens instead of "coming" pages; "Review" and "Top up" behave as in F-2.2.
- Role rules from the permission matrix apply: GRN is Full for Admin, Manager and Pharmacist and No access for Cashier; Purchase orders is Full for Admin and Manager, View for Pharmacist, and No access for Cashier.

### 3.2 Out of Scope (explicit)
- Recording a payment against a supplier and settling GRNs oldest first (Suppliers, feature F-2.4); this feature only captures what is paid at the time of receiving.
- Real printing, real export files, real barcode scanning hardware, and sending a PO to a supplier.
- Cancelling, reversing or editing a GRN after stock has been received, returns to suppliers, and credit notes.
- Supplier management (adding or editing suppliers); suppliers come from the sample data.
- Product creation from within the GRN or PO forms; products must already exist (added in F-2.2).
- Selling and picking batches at the till (earliest-expiring first at sale time).
- Settings screens; the expiry warning and reorder values come from the sample settings until F-2.5.
- Any backend, real storage or real authentication.
- Exact colour values (OQ1), numeric performance targets (OQ6) and the smallest supported width (OQ5).
- Languages other than English; multi-branch features.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)
Cross-cutting rules for every screen below (not repeated per criterion): works in light and dark themes, reflows on smaller screens, follows the design's look and content rules (plain language, `Rs. 84,250`, `21 Sep 2026`, expiry `Mar 2027` and typed as `03/2028`, money columns headed "(Rs.)" with two decimals and right-aligned, no emoji or exclamation marks), is operable by keyboard with visible focus, status always shown as a word, only one primary button per screen, and respects role access.

### 4.1 GRN list: overview and summary (S-2.3.1)
- **AC-1** — Given I open GRN, when the page loads, then I see the heading "Goods received notes" with its subtitle, the buttons Export and a primary New GRN, and three tiles in this order: Received this month, Waiting to be received, Unpaid to suppliers.
- **AC-2** — Given the sample data, when I read the tiles, then Received this month shows the rupee total of GRNs received this month with a note giving the number of GRNs and suppliers; Waiting to be received shows the number of POs still to arrive with the note "Expected within 7 days" counting those due in that period; and Unpaid to suppliers shows the rupee amount still owed on received GRNs with the number of invoices and the age of the oldest.
- **AC-3** — Given I choose Export, when it is pressed, then a clear "sample only" message appears and nothing else changes.
- **AC-4** — Given I choose New GRN, when it is pressed, then the New GRN form opens empty.
- **AC-5** — Given I receive a GRN during the session, when I return to the GRN list, then the tiles, chip counts and table reflect it.

### 4.2 GRN list: search, filters and table (S-2.3.1)
- **AC-6** — Given the GRN list, when I type in the search box, then the list narrows to GRNs whose GRN number, invoice number or supplier contains what I typed, ignoring upper/lower case, and the counts update.
- **AC-7** — Given the chips All, Draft, Received and Cancelled, when I view them, then each shows a live count matching the number of GRNs in that status, exactly one is selected at a time, and choosing one shows only those GRNs.
- **AC-8** — Given the "This month" date button, when I view the list, then it shows the date range in use and the list and tiles are based on GRNs received in that range.
- **AC-9** — Given the table, when I read a row, then I see the GRN number in the monospaced face; the received date; the supplier with "Received by <name>" underneath; the invoice number and PO number (for example "INV-88214 / PO-0148") or "No PO"; the number of items; the total in rupees; a payment badge (Paid, Part paid or Unpaid); a status badge (Draft, Received or Cancelled); and row actions with accessible names. Given nothing matches the search or filter, then I see a plain message saying no GRNs match and how to clear the filters. Given the list has 10 or fewer rows, then no pagination appears.

### 4.3 New GRN: delivery details and payment (S-2.3.2)
- **AC-10** — Given I open New GRN, when the form appears, then I see the breadcrumb "GRN > New GRN", the heading "New GRN", the subtitle with the next GRN number and "Draft saved automatically", and Save draft and a primary Receive stock.

- **AC-12** — Given the Payment group, when I read it, then it offers a payment Method (with "Credit (pay later)" as an option), Paid now in rupees and a Due date.
- **AC-13** — Given I enter a Paid now amount, when I look at the Totals, then Balance due after payment is the Total less Paid now, and a Paid now amount that is negative, not a number, or more than the Total is rejected with an error saying what to fix.
- **AC-14** — Given I leave Supplier or Supplier invoice no empty, when I choose Receive stock, then nothing is received, each problem field shows an error saying what to fix (for example "Enter the supplier invoice number"), its border turns red, and the first problem field receives focus.

### 4.4 New GRN: items and totals (S-2.3.2)
- **AC-15** — Given the Items received card, when I read it, then it offers Scan item (outline) and Add item (soft) and a table with Product (with its SKU and, when from a PO, "ordered N"), Batch no, Expiry, Received, Free, Cost, Price, Total and a remove action with an accessible name.
- **AC-16** — Given I choose Add item, when I pick a product, then a new line is added with the product's current cost and selling price filled in; given I choose Scan item, then a clear "sample only" message appears.
- **AC-17** — Given I change Received, Free or Cost on a line, when the figure changes, then the line total and the Totals (number of lines and units, Discount, Total) update immediately, and free units count in the number of units but not in the amount.
- **AC-18** — Given I choose the remove action on a line, when it is pressed, then that line is removed and the totals update.
- **AC-19** — Given a line has no batch number, an expiry that is not a valid month and year, a received quantity that is not above 0, or a negative free quantity or cost, when I choose Receive stock, then nothing is received and each problem field shows an error saying what to fix; given a GRN with no lines, then Receive stock is refused with a plain message to add an item first.
- **AC-20** — Given I choose Save draft, when it is pressed, then the GRN is kept as a Draft in the GRN list, a short confirmation is shown, and no stock, batch, cost price or PO changes. Given I have started a form and leave it, when I return to New GRN, then my in-progress entries are still there for the session.

### 4.5 Expiry warning (S-2.3.2)
- **AC-21** — Given a line's expiry is less than six months from today, when I enter it, then that expiry input gets a danger-coloured border and a warning message appears naming the product and the month, in the form "Amoxicillin 500mg expires in 5 months (02/2027). Check with the supplier before accepting."; given I correct the date to six months or more, then the border and the warning go away; given several lines are short-dated, then each is named; the warning does not stop me from receiving. An information message on the form also says that receiving adds each batch to stock and updates the cost price of the products.

### 4.6 GRN from a PO and receiving effects (S-2.3.3)
- **AC-22** — Given I choose a Purchase order in the New GRN form, when it is selected, then the supplier and one line for each ordered product (with its ordered quantity and unit cost) are filled in; only Sent and Part received POs for the chosen supplier are offered.
- **AC-23** — Given a GRN filled from a PO, when I read the lines, then I can still change quantities, costs, batch numbers and expiry, add or remove lines, and receive less than was ordered.
- **AC-24** — Given I clear the Purchase order choice or never make one, when I fill the form by hand, then I can still receive the GRN and the list shows "No PO" for it.
- **AC-25** — Given a complete, valid GRN, when I choose Receive stock, then it becomes Received, a short confirmation is shown, I am taken to the GRN list, and it appears there with its payment status worked out from what was paid.
- **AC-26** — Given I receive a GRN, when I look at the Products list and Dashboard, then each received product has a new batch with the entered batch number, expiry and quantity (received plus free), its stock has gone up by that quantity, its status is recalculated, and its cost price is the cost entered on the GRN.
- **AC-27** — Given a received product that tracks batches, when its batches are shown anywhere, then they are ordered earliest-expiring first and the batch in the Products list is the earliest-expiring one; given the product did not track batches, then stock rises with no batch shown.
- **AC-28** — Given a GRN linked to a PO is received, when I open the PO list, then the PO is Received if everything ordered has now been received, and Part received if some ordered quantity is still outstanding; a Part received PO can be received again by a later GRN until it is complete. Saving a draft GRN, or creating or sending a PO, never changes stock.

## 5. Security & Privacy Notes (business-level only)
- Sensitive data involved: supplier names, contact details and phone numbers; invoice numbers and what the shop owes each supplier; purchase and cost prices; and the quantities and expiry dates of stock. These are commercially sensitive but not customer personal data; all sample data is fictional. The signed-in person's name is recorded as "Received by".
- Who should/shouldn't be able to see or change this: only signed-in people see anything. Cashiers must not see GRNs, purchase orders, supplier balances or cost data in these areas. Pharmacists may record deliveries but may only read purchase orders. Only Admin and Manager may create, edit or send purchase orders. A person without the required access must not be able to reach a screen or action by typing its address. Receiving a delivery is the only way stock rises, so only people with GRN access can change stock.
