- **Workstream:** 2 — E-2 ShopDesk back office frontend, Feature F-2.6 Employee master (stories S-2.6.1 to S-2.6.5)
- **Traces to PRD:** FR-EMP-2, FR-EMP-3, FR-EMP-4, FR-EMP-5, FR-AUTH-5 (with FR-EMP-1 for the list it replaces, and FR-AUTH-3, FR-AUTH-4 for access). Per-AC mapping: AC-1 to AC-8: FR-EMP-3 / FR-EMP-4 (S-2.6.1, employee records and sample data); AC-9 to AC-22: FR-EMP-2 (S-2.6.2); AC-23 to AC-38: FR-EMP-3 / FR-EMP-4 (S-2.6.3); AC-39 to AC-48: FR-EMP-3 / FR-EMP-5 / FR-AUTH-5 (S-2.6.4); AC-49 to AC-53: FR-EMP-5 (S-2.6.5); AC-54 to AC-60: cross-cutting access and consistency (FR-AUTH-4, FR-AUTH-5)

## 1. Story
As a **pharmacy owner (Admin) or manager**, I want **one employee list where I can find, add, change, deactivate and export employee records, with each employee's personal details, address, pay details, allowances and photo kept together** so that **the shop has a single reliable record of its staff that Leave management and Payroll (later features) can build on, while salary information stays visible only to the people who should see it**.

## 2. Business Context
Today the Employees page shows a simple staff list. The shop needs a proper employee master: unique employee codes, contact and address details, how each person is paid (monthly or daily, with allowances, EPF and ETF choices), and whether they should also be given a sign-in. Pay details are sensitive, so who sees them is controlled. This feature serves PRD goals G1 and G3 and comes first in the HR sequence (F-2.6 Employee master, then F-2.7 Leave, then F-2.8 Payroll), because Leave and Payroll read the employees created here. The reference screens (`docs/design/hr-reference/1.png` to `4.png`) are used for layout and field content only; the ShopDesk look and feel governs styling (A8).

## 3. Scope
### 3.1 In Scope
- **S-2.6.1 Employee records and sample data.**
  - Every employee has: employee code (unique), title, first name, middle name, last name, email, date of birth, mobile number, location, address (two lines, city, suburb, state, country, zip), pay details (calculate EPF yes/no, calculate ETF yes/no, salary type Monthly or Daily, basic salary, allowance lines, over time rate), commission rate (%), product discount (%), invoice discount (%), monthly target, "Consider as Customer" yes/no, "Create user account" yes/no, four custom fields, status (Active or Inactive), and an optional photo.
  - The existing sample staff are kept and given employee codes and the new details so the list and later features are not empty. Sample employees include different salary types, some with allowances, EPF or ETF on, at least one Inactive employee, and at least one with a linked user sign-in.
  - Employee codes are unique across all employees, including Inactive ones.
- **S-2.6.2 Employee list** (replaces the Staff table on the Employees page; Attendance and Leave tabs and the stat cards stay as they are).
  - Filters: Keyword, Location, Created at (a date range) and Status, with Filter and Reset buttons.
  - Table with Image, EMP Code, Name, Address, Mobile, Email, DOB, Location, Status (as a word) and Action (edit and delete per row). An Add employee primary button, and Export CSV and Export PDF.
  - Page size selector (10 by default) and pagination with "Showing a to b of N entries".
  - Delete asks for confirmation; an employee who has a linked user sign-in or payroll history is deactivated instead of removed, and the message says so.
- **S-2.6.3 Create and Edit employee** (opened over the list, with Save and Cancel).
  - Groups: Basic details, Address, Employee Salaries and Allowances (S-2.6.4), and More Information (custom fields 1 to 4, Status, photo).
  - Required: Title, EMP Code, Location, First name, Last name and Mobile number. Status is required and defaults to Active.
  - Create user account is an optional choice. When on, saving shows a reminder to finish creating the user in Users & roles, and that employee is already chosen when the Add user panel is opened from the reminder. No account is actually created.
  - A view-only mode (same fields, nothing editable, no Save) for people with View access.
- **S-2.6.4 Salaries, Allowances and photo.**
  - Basic Salary, and a list of allowance lines, each with a name and an amount, that can be added, edited and removed.
  - These pay sections are hidden or read-only according to the person's role.
  - Employee photo: choose from the device, see a preview, replace or remove it; only JPG or PNG up to 2 MB is accepted. The photo is kept for the current session only.
- **S-2.6.5 Export.** Export CSV and Export PDF produce a file of the employees currently shown by the filters (all matching rows, not just the current page), created on the device with no upload anywhere.
- **Decisions by the user (confirmed 2026-10-17):** Employee master, Payroll and Leave are all in the overall HR scope but are delivered one feature at a time, and this story covers only the Employee master (F-2.7 Leave and F-2.8 Payroll are separate stories). All data is sample data kept only for the current session. Export and photo upload are frontend-only. Commission rate, product discount, invoice discount, monthly target and "Consider as Customer" are stored and shown only; they change nothing in other modules.
- **Accepted assumptions (taken from the Product Owner's stated defaults; none changes a confirmed requirement):**
  - A1: no new item is added to the main sidebar; the Employee list sits under the existing Employees module as the Staff tab.
  - A2 / OQ9: Employees access follows the existing matrix: Admin Full, Manager View, Pharmacist and Cashier No access. Salary and allowance information is visible and editable by Admin; Manager sees it read-only; Pharmacist and Cashier cannot reach it. Because Admin can edit roles in Users & roles (F-2.4), the permissions in force in the session always decide.
  - A3: the shop has a single location in the sample, "Main Shop"; the Location field and filter are still shown. Other locations are not added.
  - A8: the reference images give layout and fields only; styling follows ShopDesk, and money is shown as `Rs.` with two decimals.
  - OQ10: Export CSV and Export PDF create a real file from the filtered rows on the device. If a file cannot be created, a clear "sample only" message is shown instead and nothing else changes.
  - The keyword searches employee code, name, mobile and email. The Status filter offers All, Active and Inactive and starts on All. Created at is a from and to date range; either end may be left empty.
  - Title choices are Mr., Mrs., Ms., Miss and Dr.; Salary Type choices are Monthly and Daily; the toggles Calculate EPF, Calculate ETF, Consider as Customer and Create user account start Off.
  - Valid values: EMP Code is 2 to 20 letters, digits, hyphens or underscores, compared without regard to capital letters; names up to 50 characters; Mobile is nine digits (example 77XXXXXXX, spaces ignored); Email, when given, must look like an email address; Date of birth, when given, is a past date; percentages are numbers from 0 to 100; Basic Salary, Over Time Rate, Monthly Target and allowance amounts are numbers of 0 or more with up to two decimals (an allowance must be above 0 and have a name); each custom field up to 100 characters. Starting values for the numeric fields are 0.
  - The EMP Code of an existing employee can be edited, and must stay unique.
  - Page size choices are 10, 25 and 50. The list is sorted newest created first.
  - A person's sample "Can log in" or system login indication comes from the existing users; Create user account flag alone does not make an employee count as "Can log in".
  - Employees with history (linked user or payroll history) means a linked user in this phase; payroll history will apply once F-2.8 exists, and the deactivate-instead rule is already in place for it.

### 3.2 Out of Scope (explicit)
- Leave management (applying, balances, approval) — Feature F-2.7; Payroll, salary advances, nopay and payslips — Feature F-2.8. This story only stores the pay details they will read.
- Any real effect from commission rate, product discounts, invoice discounts, monthly target or "Consider as Customer" on other modules (stored only, FR-EMP-4).
- Creating a real user account from the Create user account choice (it only reminds and pre-selects in Users & roles).
- Real image storage or upload to a server; keeping photos beyond the current session.
- Additional locations, a location manager, or a branch selector.
- Changing the existing Attendance tab, the stat cards or Mark attendance behaviour (except that the Staff table is replaced by the Employee list).
- Permanent removal of employees who have history, bulk import, bulk delete, and restoring a deleted employee.
- Real loans, tax or EPF/ETF rates (these belong to Payroll).
- Exact colour values (OQ1), numeric performance targets (OQ6), the smallest supported width (OQ5); languages other than English.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)
Cross-cutting rules for every screen below (not repeated per criterion): works in light and dark themes, reflows on smaller screens, follows the ShopDesk look and content rules (plain language, `Rs. 84,250`, `21 Sep 2026`, money headed "(Rs.)" with two decimals and right-aligned, no emoji or exclamation marks), is operable by keyboard with visible focus, status always shown as a word, only one primary button per screen, error messages say what to fix, and respects role access.

### 4.1 Employee records and sample data (S-2.6.1)
- **AC-1** — Given I open the Employees page for the first time, when the Staff tab loads, then I see the existing sample staff each with an employee code, name, mobile, location and status, and at least one Inactive employee.
- **AC-2** — Given the sample data, when I read all employees, then no two have the same employee code and each has the details listed in this story (some with allowances, some Monthly and some Daily, some with EPF or ETF on).
- **AC-3** — Given I add or change an employee, when I save, then the record keeps every detail in Basic details, Address, pay details, More Information and the photo exactly as entered.
- **AC-4** — Given an employee was saved, when I reopen it, then all values, including toggles and allowance lines, show as saved.
- **AC-5** — Given I save an employee with commission rate, product discount, invoice discount, monthly target or "Consider as Customer" filled in, when I look at Reload & Utility, Products, GRN or any other module, then nothing there has changed because of these values.
- **AC-6** — Given employee details change in one place (the list, the edit form, the stat cards on Employees), when I look at the others in the same session, then names, codes, status and counts agree.
- **AC-7** — Given I refresh the page or start a new session, then employees return to the original sample set, including any added, edited, deactivated or removed ones.
- **AC-8** — Given an employee is Inactive, when I use pickers elsewhere that list people (for example the employee choice when adding a user), then Inactive employees are not offered as new choices, while their history stays visible.

### 4.2 Employee list (S-2.6.2)
- **AC-9** — Given I open the Staff tab, when it loads, then I see the filters Keyword, Location, Created at and Status with Filter and Reset buttons, the Export CSV and Export PDF buttons, a primary Add employee button, and the table with the columns Image, EMP Code, Name, Address, Mobile, Email, DOB, Location, Status and Action.
- **AC-10** — Given an employee has no photo, when I read their row, then a neutral placeholder is shown in the Image column; given an employee has a photo, then it is shown.
- **AC-11** — Given each row, when I read the Status, then it is shown as a word (Active or Inactive) with a colour, and the Action column offers edit and delete.
- **AC-12** — Given I type in Keyword and choose Filter, when the list updates, then only employees whose code, name, mobile or email contain what I typed (ignoring capital letters) are shown.
- **AC-13** — Given I choose a Location, a Created at range or a Status and choose Filter, when the list updates, then only matching employees are shown; given I use more than one filter, then they all apply together.
- **AC-14** — Given filters are set, when I choose Reset, then all filters are cleared and the full list shows again.
- **AC-15** — Given no employee matches, when the list updates, then a clear "no employees match" message is shown with a way to Reset, and the count reads "Showing 0 to 0 of 0 entries".
- **AC-16** — Given the list, when I read the foot of the table, then I see the page size selector and "Showing a to b of N entries" which is correct for the current page, filters and page size.
- **AC-17** — Given more employees than the page size, when I use pagination, then I can move between pages and the entries text updates; given I change the page size, then the list returns to the first page.
- **AC-18** — Given I choose the edit action on a row, when it is pressed, then the Edit employee form opens with that employee's saved values.
- **AC-19** — Given I choose delete on an employee with no linked user or payroll history, when I confirm, then the employee is removed from the list, a short confirmation is shown and the counts update; given I cancel, then nothing changes.
- **AC-20** — Given I choose delete on an employee who has a linked user sign-in (or payroll history), when I confirm, then the employee is made Inactive instead of removed, the message says the record was kept because it has history, and the row remains with status Inactive.
- **AC-21** — Given the delete confirmation, when it appears, then it names the employee and states what will happen.
- **AC-22** — Given I choose Add employee, when it is pressed, then an empty Create employee form opens.

### 4.3 Create and Edit employee (S-2.6.3)
- **AC-23** — Given I open the form, when I read it, then I see the groups Basic details, Address, Employee Salaries and Allowances, and More Information, with required fields marked, and the buttons Save (the one primary button) and Cancel.
- **AC-24** — Given a new employee form, when it opens, then Status is Active, the toggles are Off, the numeric fields show 0, and Location shows the shop's location.
- **AC-25** — Given I choose Save with Title, EMP Code, Location, First name, Last name or Mobile number missing, when it is pressed, then nothing is saved, each problem field shows an error saying what to fix, and the first problem field receives focus.
- **AC-26** — Given I enter an EMP Code that another employee already has (ignoring capital letters), when I save, then it is rejected with a message that the code is already in use and nothing is saved; given I keep the employee's own code when editing, then it is accepted.
- **AC-27** — Given an EMP Code that is not 2 to 20 letters, digits, hyphens or underscores, when I save, then it is rejected with a message stating the allowed format.
- **AC-28** — Given a Mobile number that is not nine digits, or an Email that does not look like an email address, when I save, then it is rejected with a message stating what is expected (the mobile example shown as 77XXXXXXX).
- **AC-29** — Given a Date of birth in the future or not a real date, when I save, then it is rejected with a message saying what to fix.
- **AC-30** — Given a percentage (Commission Rate, Product Discount, Invoice Discount) that is not a number from 0 to 100, or a Basic Salary, Over Time Rate or Monthly Target that is negative or not a number, when I save, then it is rejected with a message saying what to fix.
- **AC-31** — Given valid details, when I choose Save, then the employee is saved, the form closes, a short confirmation is shown and the list shows the new or changed row (a new employee appears at the top).
- **AC-32** — Given I choose Cancel, or close the form, when I have unsaved changes, then I am asked to confirm leaving; given I confirm, then nothing is saved; given I have no changes, then it closes straight away.
- **AC-33** — Given Calculate EPF, Calculate ETF, Consider as Customer and Create user account, when I read them, then each can be turned On or Off, and the choice is kept when saved.
- **AC-34** — Given Salary Type, when I read it, then I can choose Monthly or Daily.
- **AC-35** — Given I save a new employee with Create user account On, when saving completes, then a message reminds me to finish creating the user in Users & roles and offers a way to go there with this employee already chosen; no sign-in is created by saving.
- **AC-36** — Given an employee already has a linked user, when I edit them, then Create user account shows as already linked and cannot be turned on again; an employee never ends up with more than one user.
- **AC-37** — Given I change Status to Inactive and save, when I read the list, then the employee shows as Inactive and remains listed.
- **AC-38** — Given the Custom Fields, when I read them, then four optional text fields are shown, each with a short helper note, and what I enter is saved.

### 4.4 Salaries, Allowances and photo (S-2.6.4)
- **AC-39** — Given I am an Admin, when I open the form, then I can enter a Basic Salary and add, edit and remove allowance lines, each with a name and an amount.
- **AC-40** — Given I add an allowance line with an empty name, or an amount that is empty, zero, negative or not a number, when I save, then it is rejected with an error on that line saying what to fix.
- **AC-41** — Given allowance lines, when I remove one, then it disappears from the form straight away and is gone from the employee once saved; given I cancel the form, then the original lines remain.
- **AC-42** — Given an employee with several allowance lines, when I read the Allowances group, then I see each line and the total of all allowances in rupees with two decimals.
- **AC-43** — Given I am a Manager (View access), when I open an employee, then the Employee Salaries and Allowances information is shown read-only and cannot be changed, and no Save is offered.
- **AC-44** — Given I am a Pharmacist or Cashier, when I try to open Employees by any route, then I see the "you don't have access" message and no employee or salary information.
- **AC-45** — Given I choose an image of type JPG or PNG up to 2 MB, when I select it, then a preview appears in place of the placeholder, with a way to replace or remove it.
- **AC-46** — Given I choose a file that is not JPG or PNG, or is larger than 2 MB, when I select it, then it is rejected with a message stating the allowed types and size limit, and the previous photo (or placeholder) stays.
- **AC-47** — Given I saved an employee with a photo, when I look at the list row and reopen the employee, then the photo shows in both; given I remove it and save, then the placeholder shows again.
- **AC-48** — Given I refresh the page or start a new session, then photos chosen in this session are gone and the original sample set shows.

### 4.5 Export (S-2.6.5)
- **AC-49** — Given I choose Export CSV, when it is pressed, then a file is downloaded to my device containing every employee that matches the current filters (not only the current page), with the same columns as the list.
- **AC-50** — Given I choose Export PDF, when it is pressed, then a document is produced on my device containing the same rows and columns, with the shop's name, the date it was made and the filters used.
- **AC-51** — Given filters are set so that no employee matches, when I choose Export CSV or Export PDF, then a clear message says there is nothing to export and no file is made.
- **AC-52** — Given a file cannot be created on the device, when I choose an Export button, then a clear "sample only" message appears and nothing else changes.
- **AC-53** — Given I am a Manager (View access), when I use the list, then I can still use Export CSV and Export PDF, and the export contains no salary or allowance information.

### 4.6 Access, direct links and consistency
- **AC-54** — Given I am an Admin, when I use Employees, then Add employee, edit, delete, Export, the salary and allowance sections and photo upload are all available.
- **AC-55** — Given I am a Manager, when I use Employees, then I can see the list, open an employee read-only (including salary and allowances) and export, but Add employee, edit, delete, Save and photo change are hidden or disabled.
- **AC-56** — Given I am a Pharmacist or Cashier, when I look at the sidebar, then Employees is not shown; given I open it by typing the address, then I am stopped with the "you don't have access" message and no content.
- **AC-57** — Given I try to open the Edit employee form of an employee by typing its address without Admin access, then I am stopped (no access) or shown the view-only form according to my role, and I cannot save.
- **AC-58** — Given permissions have been changed in Users & roles, when I open Employees, then access follows the changed permissions in the same session.
- **AC-59** — Given the Employees stat cards (Total staff, Can log in and the like), when employees are added, deactivated or removed, then the counts agree with the list.
- **AC-60** — Given the same employee is shown in more than one place (the list, the edit form, the Add user choice in Users & roles, the Attendance tab), then name, code and status always agree.

## 5. Security & Privacy Notes (business-level only)
- Sensitive data involved: employees' personal details (names, date of birth, mobile number, email, home address, photo) and pay details (basic salary, allowances, over time rate, EPF and ETF choices, commission and discount rates, targets). All sample data is fictional.
- Who should/shouldn't be able to see or change this: only signed-in people see anything. Admin may see and change all employee information. Manager may see employee information and pay details read-only and may export the list, but must not change anything and the exported file must not include pay details. Pharmacist and Cashier must not see Employees or any employee information. A person without the required access must not be able to reach the list, a form or an action by typing its address. A deletion must never silently lose history, and an unsaved change must never be applied by accident. Employees without Admin approval to edit cannot save changes by any route.
