## 1. Story
As a **pharmacy owner, manager or pharmacist**, I want **a Dashboard that shows me today's sales and everything that needs my attention, and a Products area where I can find, review and add or change the medicines and items the shop sells, with stock status worked out for me** so that **I can see at a glance what to reorder, what is about to expire and what is selling, and keep the product catalogue accurate without typing stock status by hand**.

## 2. Business Context
The Dashboard is the owner's first look at the shop each morning (use case: know what needs attention) and the starting point for reordering (use case: reorder from low-stock items). The product list and product form hold the catalogue that stock-in, purchase orders and the till depend on later. This serves PRD goals G1, G2 and G4 and builds on the foundation delivered in F-2.1 (look and feel, building blocks, sample data, main frame, role access). Colour values remain approximate until the exact ones are supplied (PRD OQ1).

## 3. Scope
### 3.1 In Scope
- **S-2.2.1 Dashboard.**
  - Greeting heading with the signed-in person's first name, today's date and the shop name, with a "Daily report" button and a primary "New GRN" button.
  - Four summary tiles: Today's sales, Today's profit, Low stock and Expiring soon. The low stock and expiring figures are worked out from the product sample data.
  - A "Sales, last 7 days" bar chart with a 7 days / 30 days choice.
  - A "Needs attention" list with one action button per row (reorder low-stock items, review expiring batches, open overdue purchase orders, top up low reload float).
  - A "Low stock" table with a View all button, and a "Best sellers this week" list with horizontal bars.
- **S-2.2.2 Product list.**
  - Title and subtitle with live counts; Import CSV, Export and Add product buttons.
  - Search by name, generic name, SKU or barcode; status chips All / In stock / Low stock / Out of stock / Expiring soon with live counts; a category filter.
  - Table showing product name with its category, generic name and form, SKU, batch and expiry, stock with the minimum level, cost, price, status and row actions; "Showing a–b of N products" and pagination.
  - Stock status is always worked out from stock, reorder level and nearest batch expiry, never typed in.
  - Batch-tracked products show their batches earliest-expiring first; products with batch tracking off show a single stock figure.
- **S-2.2.3 Add / Edit product.**
  - Breadcrumb, Cancel and Save product at the top; a form in groups for Basic details, Pricing and Stock rules; a footer with Cancel, Save & add another and Save product.
  - A side area with a live Margin summary, a Status summary with its two switches and note, and a tip about receiving stock through a GRN.
  - Required fields (Product name, Category, Selling price) are checked with errors that say what to fix; the barcode field offers a scan button that is sample only.
- Accepted assumptions (consistent with F-2.1 and PRD open questions):
  - OQ4: Daily report, Import CSV, Export and the barcode scan button are shown but do nothing real and give a clear "sample only" message.
  - Sales, profit and best-seller figures come from sample data and are not recalculated from real sales.
  - The "Top up" action and the "New GRN", "Create PO" and overdue purchase order actions take the person to the relevant area; where that area is not built yet (stock-in, purchase orders, reload) it opens as its "coming" page inside the normal frame until features F-2.3 and F-2.5 are delivered.
  - The expiry alert window and default reorder level come from the sample settings (60 days and 20 units) until Settings is delivered in F-2.5.
  - Row actions on the product list are view, edit and more; "view" and "edit" open the product form for that item (read-only for people with View access), and "more" offers no real actions beyond a "sample only" message.
- Role rules from the permission matrix apply: the Dashboard is reachable by Admin, Manager (Full) and Pharmacist (View), and not by Cashier; Products is Full for Admin, Manager and Pharmacist and View for Cashier.

### 3.2 Out of Scope (explicit)
- Real sales, profit or best-seller calculation from real bills, and real reports; the Daily report is a sample-only button.
- Creating batches or recording received stock; batches and expiry dates come in through GRN (feature F-2.3).
- CSV import or export file generation, printing, and real barcode scanning hardware.
- Bulk actions on selected product rows beyond showing the selection tick boxes.
- Deleting products, and any destructive product actions.
- The content of the GRN, Purchase order, Suppliers, Employees, Reload & Utility, Users and Settings screens (features F-2.3 to F-2.5).
- Any backend, real storage or real authentication; data lives only for the current session.
- Exact colour values (OQ1), numeric performance targets (OQ6) and the smallest supported width (OQ5).
- Languages other than English; multi-branch features.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)
Cross-cutting rules for every screen below (not repeated per criterion): works in light and dark themes, reflows on smaller screens, follows the design's look and content rules (plain language, `Rs. 84,250`, `21 Sep 2026`, expiry `Mar 2027`, no emoji or exclamation marks), is operable by keyboard with visible focus, and respects role access.

### 4.1 Dashboard heading and actions (S-2.2.1)
- **AC-1** — Given I am signed in as someone with Dashboard access, when I open the Dashboard, then I see a heading "Good morning, <my first name>" with today's date and "Main Shop".
- **AC-2** — Given the Dashboard, when I look at the heading actions, then I see an outline "Daily report" button and a primary "New GRN" button, and there is only one primary button on the screen.
- **AC-3** — Given I choose "Daily report", when it is pressed, then a clear "sample only" message appears and nothing else changes.
- **AC-4** — Given I choose "New GRN", when it is pressed, then I am taken to the stock-in area (or its "coming" page while that feature is not built).
- **AC-5** — Given I am a Pharmacist (View access to the Dashboard), when I open it, then I can read everything but any button that would create or change data is hidden or disabled, while navigation to other areas I am allowed to open still works.

### 4.2 Summary tiles (S-2.2.1)
- **AC-6** — Given the Dashboard, when I view the top row, then I see four tiles: Today's sales, Today's profit, Low stock and Expiring soon, in that order, each with a figure, a short note and an icon.
- **AC-7** — Given the sample data, when I read the first two tiles, then Today's sales shows a rupee amount with a comparison note against last Monday, and Today's profit shows a rupee amount with its margin percentage and the number of bills.
- **AC-8** — Given the product sample data, when I read the Low stock tile, then it shows the number of items at or below their reorder level and a note with how many of those are out of stock, and the numbers match the product list's status counts.
- **AC-9** — Given the product sample data, when I read the Expiring soon tile, then it shows the number of batches expiring within the alert window (60 days by default) and says so in its note.
- **AC-10** — Given I change product data during the session (for example save a product with different stock), when I return to the Dashboard, then the Low stock and Expiring soon figures reflect the change.

### 4.3 Sales chart (S-2.2.1)
- **AC-11** — Given the Dashboard, when I view the sales chart, then it is titled "Sales, last 7 days", shows one bar per day in a single colour, and the values are in rupees (thousands) for all bills.
- **AC-12** — Given the chart, when I look at the bars, then today's bar is darker than the others, and only the highest day and today carry a value label.
- **AC-13** — Given the chart, when I choose "30 days" then the chart shows the last 30 days and its title updates to match; when I choose "7 days" it returns to 7 days; exactly one choice is selected at a time and the selected one is clearly marked.
- **AC-14** — Given the chart, when I use only the keyboard or a screen reader, then I can change the period and I can read each day's value as text (not only by colour or bar height).

### 4.4 Needs attention (S-2.2.1)
- **AC-15** — Given the sample data, when I view "Needs attention", then I see a row for each of: low-stock items to reorder (with the count), batches expiring soon (with the count and the date they expire by), overdue purchase orders (with the count), and a low reload float.
- **AC-16** — Given each row, when I look at it, then it has exactly one action button: "Create PO" for low stock, "Review" for expiring batches, "Open" for overdue purchase orders, and "Top up" for low float.
- **AC-17** — Given I choose "Create PO", when it is pressed, then I go to the purchase order area for creating an order; "Review" goes to the product list showing expiring items; "Open" goes to the purchase orders area; "Top up" goes to the reload area; where a destination is not built yet I see its "coming" page.
- **AC-18** — Given a role that cannot open the destination of an action (for example no access), when I view that row, then the action is hidden or disabled and I am never taken to a blocked page by the button.
- **AC-19** — Given nothing needs attention (for example no low stock, expiring batches, overdue orders or low float), when I view the list, then it shows a plain message saying nothing needs attention right now.

### 4.5 Low stock table and best sellers (S-2.2.1)
- **AC-20** — Given the Dashboard, when I view "Low stock", then a table shows for each product its name, stock on hand, reorder level and a status badge with a word, with numbers right-aligned.
- **AC-21** — Given the Low stock table, when I choose "View all", then I go to the product list filtered to low stock items.
- **AC-22** — Given more products are low than fit in the table, when I view it, then the table shows only the most urgent few (out of stock first, then the lowest stock) and "View all" is the way to see the rest.
- **AC-23** — Given the Dashboard, when I view "Best sellers this week", then I see the top products with their units sold and a horizontal bar for each, longest for the best seller, with the unit count also written as a number.
- **AC-24** — Given a narrow screen, when I view the Dashboard, then tiles, chart, list, table and best sellers reflow into fewer columns without overlap or sideways page scrolling.

### 4.6 Product list: overview and filtering (S-2.2.2)
- **AC-25** — Given I open Products, when the page loads, then I see the heading "Products", a subtitle of the form "312 products · 14 low stock · 6 expiring soon" with the live numbers, and the buttons Import CSV, Export and a primary Add product.
- **AC-26** — Given I choose Import CSV or Export, when it is pressed, then a clear "sample only" message appears and nothing else changes.
- **AC-27** — Given the product list, when I type in the search box, then the list narrows to products whose name, generic name, SKU or barcode contains what I typed, ignoring upper/lower case, and the counts and "Showing" summary update.
- **AC-28** — Given the status chips All, In stock, Low stock, Out of stock and Expiring soon, when I view them, then each shows a live count that matches the number of products in that status, and exactly one chip is selected at a time.
- **AC-29** — Given I choose a status chip, when the list updates, then only products with that status are shown and the first page is shown.
- **AC-30** — Given the category filter, when I choose a category, then only products in that category are shown; when I choose all categories, the filter is cleared.
- **AC-31** — Given a search, a status chip and a category are all set, when the list updates, then only products matching all three are shown, and the chip counts reflect the current search and category.
- **AC-32** — Given the filters match nothing, when the list updates, then I see a plain message saying no products match and how to clear the filters (not an empty table).
- **AC-33** — Given I arrive from the Dashboard's Low stock "View all" or the Expiring soon "Review", when the list opens, then the matching status chip is already selected.

### 4.7 Product list: table, status and batches (S-2.2.2)
- **AC-34** — Given the product table, when I read a row, then I see a selection tick box; the product name with its category, generic name and form underneath; the SKU in the monospaced face; batch and expiry; stock with "min N" underneath; cost and price in rupees (headed "(Rs.)", two decimals, right-aligned); a status badge; and view, edit and more row actions each with an accessible name.
- **AC-35** — Given a product with stock of 0, when its status is shown, then it is "Out of stock" regardless of anything else.
- **AC-36** — Given a product with stock above 0 and at or below its reorder level, when its status is shown, then it is "Low stock".
- **AC-37** — Given a product whose nearest batch expires within the alert window (60 days by default) and which is not out of stock or low stock, when its status is shown, then it is "Expiring soon".
- **AC-38** — Given a product that is none of the above, when its status is shown, then it is "In stock".
- **AC-39** — Given a product that is both low stock and has a batch expiring soon, when its status is shown, then a single status is shown according to this order of priority: Out of stock, then Low stock, then Expiring soon, then In stock (for example a low-stock product with a batch expiring soon shows Low stock). (FR-PROD-2)
- **AC-40** — Given the status, when I try to edit a product, then there is nowhere to type or pick a status; it can only change because stock, reorder level or batch expiry changed.
- **AC-41** — Given a product that tracks batches, when its row shows batch and expiry, then it shows the batch with the earliest expiry, written like "B2405 / Exp Mar 2027"; given a product with batch tracking off, then it shows no batch and no expiry (a dash) and a single stock figure.
- **AC-42** — Given a product with several batches, when its batches are used or shown anywhere in this feature, then they are ordered earliest-expiring first, each with batch number, expiry and quantity; there is no separate product details screen (see AC-48).
- **AC-43** — Given the status badges, when they are shown, then each has a word and the right tone (In stock success, Low stock and Expiring soon warning, Out of stock danger), never colour alone.

### 4.8 Product list: pagination, selection and access (S-2.2.2)
- **AC-44** — Given more than 10 matching products, when I view the table, then it shows 10 per page with the footer "Showing a–b of N products" and pagination I can use to move between pages; given 10 or fewer, then no pagination appears.
- **AC-45** — Given I move to another page and then change a filter or search, when the list updates, then I am returned to the first page.
- **AC-46** — Given I tick one or more product rows, when I look at the table, then the ticks show clearly, and a header tick box selects or clears all rows on the current page; no bulk action is offered in this feature.
- **AC-47** — Given I am a Cashier or any role with View access to Products, when I open the list, then I can search, filter and read everything, but Add product and edit actions are hidden or disabled.
- **AC-48** — Given I choose "view" or "edit" on a row, when it is pressed, then the product form opens with that product's current values filled in (there is no separate details screen); given I have only View access, then the form opens read-only with fields disabled and no Save actions; given I choose "more", then a "sample only" message appears. (FR-PROD-7)
- **AC-48a** — Given I am a Cashier, when I read the product list, then I can see each product's cost and price, and the cost and margin visible to me are as shown in the list. (FR-PROD-6)
- **AC-49** — Given a narrow screen, when I view the product list, then the table scrolls sideways or stacks without losing the product name, stock, status and row actions, and the search, chips and category filter remain usable.

### 4.9 Add / Edit product form (S-2.2.3)
- **AC-50** — Given I choose Add product, when the form opens, then I see a breadcrumb "Products > Add product", the heading "Add product" with the subtitle "Fill in what you know now; batches and expiry come in through GRN.", and Cancel and a primary Save product at the top.
- **AC-51** — Given I choose edit on an existing product, when the form opens, then the heading says it is editing that product, the breadcrumb shows the product's name, and every field shows its current value.
- **AC-52** — Given the form, when I read it, then it has the groups Basic details (Product name, Unit, Generic name, Category, Manufacturer, SKU, Barcode), Pricing (Cost price, Selling price, Tax) and Stock rules (Reorder level, Opening stock, Shelf or location, and the switches "Track batches and expiry dates" and "Prescription required"), each group with a short side label and description, and the required fields marked.
- **AC-53** — Given the Barcode field, when I view it, then it shows the hint "Point the scanner at the field and scan." and a scan button with an accessible name; when I press the scan button, then a "sample only" message appears.
- **AC-54** — Given Cost price and Selling price show "Rs.", Reorder level and Opening stock show the unit (such as "tablets"), and Opening stock shows the hint "Leave 0 and use GRN instead.", when I view the form, then these prefixes, suffixes and hints appear as designed.
- **AC-55** — Given the form footer, when I look at it, then it offers Cancel, Save & add another and a primary Save product, and the top Save product and the footer Save product do the same thing.
- **AC-56** — Given I leave Product name, Category or Selling price empty (or enter a selling price that is not a positive amount), when I choose Save product, then nothing is saved, each problem field is marked with an error that says what to fix (for example "Enter the product name"), the border turns red, and the first problem field receives focus.
- **AC-57** — Given I enter a cost price, reorder level or opening stock that is negative or not a number, when I save, then I see an error saying what to fix and nothing is saved.
- **AC-58** — Given I enter a SKU that already belongs to another product, when I save, then I see an error saying that SKU is already used and nothing is saved. (FR-PROD-5)
- **AC-59** — Given valid details, when I choose Save product, then the product is saved for the session, I am returned to the product list, a short confirmation is shown, and the product appears in the list with its status worked out (for example "Out of stock" with opening stock 0).
- **AC-60** — Given valid details, when I choose Save & add another, then the product is saved, a short confirmation is shown, and I stay on a fresh empty form ready for the next product.
- **AC-61** — Given I have changed anything and choose Cancel, when I confirm, then nothing is saved and I return to the product list; if I changed nothing, I return without a prompt.
- **AC-62** — Given I save an edited product, when I return to the list and to the Dashboard, then the changed values and the recalculated status and counts are shown.
- **AC-63** — Given I add a product with "Track batches and expiry dates" on and an opening stock above 0, when it is saved, then the stock is recorded and the list shows no batch or expiry until a GRN records one. (FR-PROD-3)

### 4.10 Margin, status card and tip (S-2.2.3)
- **AC-64** — Given I type a cost price and a selling price, when either changes, then the Margin summary updates immediately showing the margin percentage (for example "30%"), the profit per unit in rupees using the unit chosen (for example "Rs. 0.90 profit per tablet"), and a progress bar that fills to match the percentage.
- **AC-65** — Given the cost price is empty, zero, or higher than the selling price, when I view the Margin summary, then it shows a neutral message (no margin yet, or a loss clearly worded in words) and never shows a broken figure such as "NaN" or "Infinity".
- **AC-66** — Given the Status summary, when I view it, then it has two switches, "Active, can be sold" and "Show in POS quick list", each with a visible on or off state and label, and the note "Inactive products stay in reports but disappear from sales and new GRNs."
- **AC-67** — Given I switch "Active, can be sold" off and save, when I return to the list, then the product is still listed and its inactive state is clear; it is not hidden or removed. (FR-PROD-5)
- **AC-68** — Given the form, when I view the side area, then there is an information tip explaining that after saving I should receive stock with a GRN so the first batch and expiry date are recorded.
- **AC-69** — Given a narrow screen, when I view the form, then the fields reflow into fewer columns, the side summary moves below the main form, and the footer buttons stay reachable.

### 4.11 Access, direct links and consistency (S-2.2.1 to S-2.2.3)
- **AC-70** — Given I am a Cashier, when I open the Dashboard by typing its address, then I see the "you don't have access" message and no Dashboard content, as defined in F-2.1; the Dashboard item is not in my sidebar.
- **AC-71** — Given I am a Cashier or any role with View access to Products, when I open the Add product or Edit product address directly, then I am stopped with the "you don't have access" message and no form is shown.
- **AC-72** — Given the Products count badge in the sidebar, when I add a product, then the count updates to match the number of products.
- **AC-73** — Given the same product, when I see its name, stock and status on the Dashboard's Low stock table and on the product list, then they always agree.
- **AC-74** — Given I refresh the page or start a new session, when I return to the Dashboard and Products, then the data is back to the original sample set (changes live only for the session).

## 5. Security & Privacy Notes (business-level only)
- Sensitive data involved: product cost prices and margins, sales and profit figures, and stock levels. These are commercially sensitive but not personal data; all sample data is fictional. The signed-in person's first name is shown in the Dashboard greeting.
- Who should/shouldn't be able to see or change this: only signed-in people see anything. Cashiers must not see the Dashboard, sales or profit figures. Cashiers may read the product list but must not add or change products. Cashiers can see product cost and margin on the Products list (confirmed requirement FR-PROD-6; whether to hide cost from Cashiers is a possible later stakeholder decision, with no change now). People with View access can read but never create or change. A person without the required access must not be able to reach a screen or action by typing its address.
