# User Story 2.7 — Leave management

- **Workstream:** 2 — E-2 ShopDesk back office frontend, Feature F-2.7 Leave management (stories S-2.7.1 to S-2.7.3)
- **Traces to PRD:** FR-LVE-1, FR-LVE-2, FR-LVE-3, FR-EMP-1 (with FR-AUTH-3, FR-AUTH-4 for access). Per-AC mapping: AC-1 to AC-10: FR-LVE-2 / FR-EMP-1 (S-2.7.1, leave types, entitlements, balances, requests and On leave counts); AC-11 to AC-33: FR-LVE-1 / FR-LVE-2 (S-2.7.2, leave application and Leave Balance); AC-34 to AC-46: FR-LVE-3 (S-2.7.3, approval and the Leave tab); AC-47 to AC-52: cross-cutting access and consistency (FR-AUTH-4, FR-EMP-1)
- **Status:** Ready
- **Author:** Thilina (BA)
- **Created:** 2026-10-19

## 1. Story
As a **pharmacy owner (Admin) or manager**, I want **to record an employee's leave request for a chosen leave year, see each employee's leave balance by leave type, and approve or reject pending requests** so that **the shop knows who is away and when, leave entitlements are tracked fairly, and the Employees "On leave" counts and the later Payroll feature can rely on accurate leave records**.

## 2. Business Context
After the Employee master (F-2.6), the shop needs to track staff leave. Today the Employees page has a Leave tab that only lists sample leave. This feature adds leave types with yearly entitlements, a leave application page with a Leave Balance table beside it, and an approval step. It serves PRD goals G1 and G3 and sits second in the HR sequence (F-2.6 Employee master, F-2.7 Leave management, F-2.8 Payroll), because Payroll reads leave. The reference image (`docs/design/hr-reference/9.png`) is used for layout and field content only; the ShopDesk look and feel governs styling (A8).

## 3. Scope
### 3.1 In Scope
- **S-2.7.1 Leave types, entitlements, balances and requests (sample data).**
  - Leave types as seen in the reference: Annual Leave, Carry Forward - Annual Leave, Casual Leave, Christmas Day, Medical Leave.
  - Each employee has a yearly entitlement for each leave type, in days.
  - Each leave request has: employee, leave type, from date, to date, number of days, reason, comments, optional attachment name, covering employee, approver, approver comment, and a status of Pending, Approved, Rejected or Cancelled.
  - Balance for an employee, leave type and year = Entitlement minus Utilized minus Pending Approval (A4). Applying adds days to Pending Approval; approval moves them to Utilized; rejection or cancellation releases them.
  - Sample data covers requests in each status, leave in more than one year, and at least one employee who is away today.
  - The Employees "On leave" count and the "On leave" filter read from approved leave.
- **S-2.7.2 Leave application page** (reached from the Employees area; same page shows the Leave Balance table).
  - Fields: Employee (who the leave is for), Leave Year with its period shown (for example 01/01/2026 to 31/12/2026), Leave Type, From Date, To Date, Attachment (Browse), Reason for Leave (a list), Comments, Covering Employee (with Search employees), Approver, and the buttons Apply and Reset.
  - Required: Employee, Leave Type, From Date, To Date, Reason for Leave and Approver.
  - Validation: To Date before From Date, overlap with another leave of the same employee, days requested above the balance (the message says how many days are left), covering employee being the same as the applicant, and dates outside the selected leave year.
  - Leave Balance table for the chosen employee and year: Leave Type, Entitlement, Balance, Utilized, Pending Approval.
- **S-2.7.3 Leave approval and the Leave tab.**
  - The chosen approver, or an Admin, can approve or reject a Pending request with an optional comment.
  - An Admin can cancel a Pending request, or an Approved request that has not yet started.
  - Balances, Pending Approval and the On leave counts update straight away.
  - The existing Leave tab on the Employees page lists the leave requests, shows status as a word, and offers the approve, reject and cancel actions where allowed.
- **Decisions by the user (confirmed 2026-10-17):** HR scope is delivered one feature at a time; this story covers Leave management only. All data is sample data kept only for the current session; frontend-only. The Attachment is a mock choice (a file name is shown; nothing is uploaded).
- **Accepted assumptions (taken from the Product Owner's stated defaults; none changes a confirmed requirement; the Product Owner is asked to confirm them, see Open Items):**
  - A4: balance formula as above. The reference table's sample figures do not follow it exactly; sample data in this story is set so that it does.
  - A5: approval is included because Approver and Pending Approval imply it.
  - Placement (A1): no new item in the main sidebar; the Leave application page and the Leave tab sit under the existing Employees area.
  - A8: reference image gives layout and fields only; styling follows ShopDesk; dates are shown like `21 Sep 2026`, days with two decimals like `3.00`.
  - Access follows the existing matrix: Admin Full, Manager View, Pharmacist and Cashier No access. Applying, resetting, cancelling and changing entitlements are Admin actions. Manager can view requests and balances read-only. Because Admin can edit roles in Users & roles (F-2.4), the permissions in force in the session always decide.
  - Who is the applicant: because the reference shows no employee choice and the session user may not be an employee, the page includes an Employee field listing Active employees. It starts empty and the Leave Balance table shows once an employee is chosen.
  - Approving or rejecting: Admin may always act. A person whose sign-in is linked to the chosen approver may also act on that request, provided they can open the Employees area. A person never approves their own request.
  - Approver choices are Active employees who are Admin or Manager staff, and never the applicant. The sample approver in the reference ("1201 - Pradeep Sanjeewa") is only an example.
  - Leave Year choices are the previous, current and next year; the current year is selected at start. A leave year runs 1 January to 31 December. A request must fall entirely inside the chosen leave year; a request that spans two years is entered as two requests.
  - Days are counted as calendar days from From Date to To Date inclusive (no half days, no weekend or holiday exclusion, in line with the whole-day entitlements in the reference). A single day is From Date equal to To Date.
  - Leave Type choices include all five types; a type whose balance is 0 or less still shows but cannot be applied for. Yearly entitlements in the sample: Annual Leave 14, Carry Forward - Annual Leave 0, Casual Leave 7, Christmas Day 1, Medical Leave 7 days (in whole days, shown with two decimals).
  - Reason for Leave list choices: Personal, Medical, Family, Travel, Bereavement, Other.
  - Comments up to 250 characters. Attachment accepts PDF, JPG or PNG up to 2 MB (the same limit as photos), is optional and is kept for the current session only.
  - Past dates are allowed for From Date (leave recorded after the fact); an overlap check applies only to Pending and Approved requests, so Rejected and Cancelled requests do not block dates.
  - Covering Employee is optional, must be an Active employee other than the applicant, and Search employees filters the list by code or name. A covering employee who is themselves on approved leave on any of those dates is not blocked but a warning is shown.
  - An employee on approved leave covering today counts as "On leave" in the Employees counts and filter. The employees already shown as On leave in the sample are given matching approved leave so counts stay consistent.
  - Applying leave for an Inactive employee is not possible; leave history of an employee who later becomes Inactive stays visible.
  - The Leave tab is sorted by From Date, newest first, with the same page size choices as the Employee list (10, 25, 50).
  - Apply on success clears the form (except Employee and Leave Year) and shows a short confirmation; the new request is Pending.

### 3.2 Out of Scope (explicit)
- Payroll, salary advances, nopay and payslips, and the quick "Create Leave" from a payroll row (FR-LVE-4, OQ11), which belong to Feature F-2.8 (S-2.8.3).
- Editing a request after it is applied (a request is cancelled and re-entered), editing entitlements or leave types from the screen, carry-forward calculation between years, accrual rules, half-day or hourly leave, public-holiday calendars.
- Notifications or emails to approvers, covering employees or applicants.
- Real file upload or storage of attachments; keeping any leave data beyond the current session.
- Changing the Employee master, the Attendance tab or Mark attendance behaviour (except that On leave counts now read from approved leave).
- Exact colour values (OQ1), numeric performance targets (OQ6), the smallest supported width (OQ5); languages other than English.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)
Cross-cutting rules for every screen below (not repeated per criterion): works in light and dark themes, reflows on smaller screens, follows the ShopDesk look and content rules (plain language, `21 Sep 2026`, no emoji or exclamation marks), is operable by keyboard with visible focus, status always shown as a word, only one primary button per screen, error messages say what to fix, and respects role access.

### 4.1 Leave types, entitlements, balances and requests (S-2.7.1)
- **AC-1** — Given I open leave information for an employee and year, when it loads, then I see the five leave types Annual Leave, Carry Forward - Annual Leave, Casual Leave, Christmas Day and Medical Leave, each with an Entitlement, Balance, Utilized and Pending Approval in days.
- **AC-2** — Given any employee, leave type and year, when I read the figures, then Balance equals Entitlement minus Utilized minus Pending Approval.
- **AC-3** — Given the sample data, when I read the leave requests, then there is at least one request in each of Pending, Approved, Rejected and Cancelled, requests in more than one leave year, and each request shows employee, leave type, dates, days, reason, approver and status.
- **AC-4** — Given a Pending request, when I read the balance, then its days are counted in Pending Approval and are already taken off the Balance.
- **AC-5** — Given an Approved request, then its days are counted as Utilized and not as Pending Approval; given a Rejected or Cancelled request, then its days are in neither and the Balance is as if it were never applied.
- **AC-6** — Given an employee has approved leave that includes today, when I look at the Employees stat card "On leave" and the "On leave" filter, then that employee is counted and listed.
- **AC-7** — Given an employee's approved leave does not include today, or is Pending, Rejected or Cancelled, when I look at the On leave count, then that employee is not counted because of it.
- **AC-8** — Given the sample data, when I open Employees, then the employees already shown as On leave have matching approved leave and the stat cards and list agree.
- **AC-9** — Given I refresh the page or start a new session, then leave requests, balances and counts return to the original sample set, including any applied, approved, rejected or cancelled ones.
- **AC-10** — Given leave is changed in one place (the application page, the Leave tab, the stat cards), when I look at the others in the same session, then requests, balances, statuses and counts agree.

### 4.2 Leave application page (S-2.7.2)
- **AC-11** — Given I open the Leave application page, when it loads, then I see Employee, Leave Year (with its period, for example 01/01/2026 to 31/12/2026), Leave Type, From Date, To Date, Attachment with a Browse choice, Reason for Leave, Comments, Covering Employee with Search employees, Approver, and the buttons Apply (the one primary button) and Reset, with required fields marked.
- **AC-12** — Given I have not chosen an employee, when the page loads, then the Leave Balance table asks me to choose an employee; given I choose one, then the table shows that employee's balances for the chosen year.
- **AC-13** — Given I change the Leave Year, when it updates, then the period text and the Leave Balance table show the chosen year.
- **AC-14** — Given I choose Apply with Employee, Leave Type, From Date, To Date, Reason for Leave or Approver missing, when it is pressed, then nothing is saved, each problem field shows an error saying what to fix, and the first problem field receives focus.
- **AC-15** — Given a To Date earlier than the From Date, when I apply, then it is rejected with a message saying the To Date cannot be before the From Date.
- **AC-16** — Given From Date or To Date outside the chosen Leave Year, when I apply, then it is rejected with a message saying the dates must be inside that leave year.
- **AC-17** — Given the dates overlap a Pending or Approved leave of the same employee, when I apply, then it is rejected with a message naming the overlapping dates; given the overlap is only with a Rejected or Cancelled request, then it is accepted.
- **AC-18** — Given the days requested are more than the balance of the chosen leave type, when I apply, then it is rejected with a message saying how many days are left (for example "Only 3.00 days of Casual Leave left").
- **AC-19** — Given a leave type whose balance is 0 or less, when I read the Leave Type choices, then it is still listed but I cannot apply for it, and the reason is shown.
- **AC-20** — Given I choose the applicant as the Covering Employee, when I apply, then it is rejected with a message saying the covering employee must be someone else; given the covering employee is left empty, then it is accepted.
- **AC-21** — Given the Covering Employee list, when I use Search employees, then only Active employees whose code or name contain what I typed (ignoring capital letters) are listed, and the applicant is not listed.
- **AC-22** — Given the Approver list, when I read it, then it lists only Active Admin or Manager staff and not the applicant.
- **AC-23** — Given the chosen covering employee has approved leave on any of the requested dates, when I apply, then the request is still accepted and a clear warning says so.
- **AC-24** — Given I choose an attachment of type PDF, JPG or PNG up to 2 MB, when I select it, then its file name is shown with a way to remove it; given the file type or size is not allowed, then it is rejected with a message stating the allowed types and size limit and the previous choice stays.
- **AC-25** — Given Comments longer than 250 characters, when I apply, then it is rejected with a message stating the limit.
- **AC-26** — Given valid details, when I choose Apply, then a Pending request is created for those dates and days, a short confirmation is shown, and the form clears except Employee and Leave Year.
- **AC-27** — Given I applied, when I read the Leave Balance table, then Pending Approval has increased and Balance has decreased by the days requested, straight away.
- **AC-28** — Given From Date equal to To Date, when I apply, then one day is requested; given several dates, then the days are the calendar days from From Date to To Date inclusive, and the number of days is shown before I apply.
- **AC-29** — Given I choose Reset, when it is pressed with unsaved entries, then I am asked to confirm; given I confirm, then all entries are cleared (Leave Year returns to the current year) and nothing is saved; given the form is empty, then it clears straight away.
- **AC-30** — Given I leave the page with unsaved entries, when I try to navigate away, then I am asked to confirm leaving.
- **AC-31** — Given an Inactive employee, when I read the Employee choices, then they are not offered; given their leave history exists, then it stays visible in the Leave tab.
- **AC-32** — Given the Leave Balance table, when I read it, then Entitlement, Balance, Utilized and Pending Approval are right-aligned days with two decimals, and the leave type names are shown in full.
- **AC-33** — Given there are no leave types or no employees to choose, when the page loads, then a clear empty message is shown instead of a blank form.

### 4.3 Leave approval and the Leave tab (S-2.7.3)
- **AC-34** — Given I open the Leave tab, when it loads, then I see the leave requests with employee, leave type, from and to dates, days, reason, approver and status (shown as a word with a colour), sorted by From Date, newest first, with the page size choices 10, 25 and 50 and "Showing a to b of N entries".
- **AC-35** — Given the Leave tab, when I read the filter chips or search, then I can narrow requests by status (All, Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled) and by employee name or code, and an empty result shows a clear "no leave requests match" message with a way to reset.
- **AC-36** — Given a Pending request and I am an Admin, when I read its row, then I can Approve or Reject it.
- **AC-37** — Given a Pending request and I am signed in as the person linked to its chosen approver, when I read its row, then I can Approve or Reject it; given I am not the approver and not an Admin, then I cannot.
- **AC-38** — Given a Pending request that belongs to me (I am the applicant), when I read its row, then I cannot approve or reject it.
- **AC-39** — Given I choose Approve, when I confirm with or without a comment, then the status becomes Approved, the days move from Pending Approval to Utilized, a short confirmation is shown, and the comment is kept with the request.
- **AC-40** — Given I choose Reject, when I confirm with or without a comment, then the status becomes Rejected, the days are released back to the Balance, and the comment is kept with the request.
- **AC-41** — Given the approve or reject confirmation, when it appears, then it names the employee, dates and days, has an optional comment (up to 250 characters), and can be dismissed without any change.
- **AC-42** — Given an Approved request whose dates include today, when approval completes, then the employee is counted as On leave in the Employees stat cards and filter straight away.
- **AC-43** — Given a request that is already Approved, Rejected or Cancelled, when I read its row, then it cannot be approved or rejected again.
- **AC-44** — Given I am an Admin and a request is Pending, or Approved but not yet started, when I choose Cancel and confirm, then the status becomes Cancelled and its days are released back to the Balance; given the leave has already started or ended, then Cancel is not offered.
- **AC-45** — Given a request that has been decided, when I open its details, then I can see the approver, the decision comment and the status as read-only text.
- **AC-46** — Given an action cannot be completed (for example the request changed meanwhile), when I confirm, then a clear message says what happened and nothing is changed.

### 4.4 Access, direct links and consistency
- **AC-47** — Given I am an Admin, when I use Leave, then applying, resetting, approving, rejecting and cancelling are all available.
- **AC-48** — Given I am a Manager (View access), when I use Leave, then I can see the Leave tab, the Leave application page's Leave Balance table and the request details, but Apply, Reset, Cancel and (unless I am the chosen approver) Approve and Reject are hidden or disabled.
- **AC-49** — Given I am a Pharmacist or Cashier, when I look at the sidebar, then Employees is not shown; given I open any leave page by typing its address, then I am stopped with the "you don't have access" message and no leave information.
- **AC-50** — Given permissions have been changed in Users & roles, when I open Leave, then access follows the changed permissions in the same session.
- **AC-51** — Given I try to apply, approve, reject or cancel by typing an address or any route other than the screen controls, without the required access, then it is not allowed and nothing changes.
- **AC-52** — Given the same employee or request is shown in more than one place (Leave tab, application page, Employees counts, Add user choice), then name, code, status and day figures always agree.

## 5. Security & Privacy Notes (business-level only)
- Sensitive data involved: employees' leave dates, leave reasons (which may reveal medical or family matters), comments and attachments, and leave balances. All sample data is fictional.
- Who should/shouldn't be able to see or change this: only signed-in people see anything. Admin may see and change all leave information. Manager may see leave information read-only, and may approve or reject a request only when they are the chosen approver. Pharmacist and Cashier must not see leave information. A person without the required access must not be able to reach a leave page or perform a leave action by typing its address. Nobody may approve their own request. A decision must never be applied by accident (confirmation first), and a decided request must never silently change again.
