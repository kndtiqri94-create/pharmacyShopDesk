# User Story 2.12 — POS Payment Panel Redesign and Bank Accounts

- **Workstream:** 2 — F-2.12 POS payment panel redesign and bank accounts (S-2.12.1, S-2.12.2, S-2.12.3)
- **Traces to PRD:** FR-POS-21, FR-POS-22, FR-POS-23, FR-POS-8, FR-POS-8a, FR-POS-9 (withdrawn), FR-POS-17, FR-FND-7
- **Status:** Ready
- **Author:** Thilina (BA)
- **Created:** 2026-11-10

## 1. Story
As a **cashier (and as an Admin, Manager or Pharmacist taking payment at the counter)**, I want **a clearer payment area on the billing screen that shows the bill summary and a prominent Total, lets me enter Cash, Card, Online or Credit payments with the right details for each, and lets me record the shop's bank account for an Online payment (adding a new account on the spot if needed)** so that **I can take mixed payments quickly and accurately, and every Online payment is traceable to the bank account that received it**.

## 2. Business Context
The payment side of the billing screen is where the cashier finishes every sale. The shop supplied four reference screenshots as a style and layout guide (not a pixel copy): Cash, Card, Online, and an "add bank account" form. The redesign makes the figures easier to read, makes each payment method ask only for what it needs, and links Online payments to a bank account so the shop can later tell which account received the money. The on-screen number keypad is removed; amounts are typed on the keyboard. The reference screenshots show a "More" payment option; the user decided to ignore it, so the payment methods stay exactly Cash, Card, Online and Credit. This continues the colour-coded button restyle from workstream 2.11. It serves PRD sections 5.9.5 and 5.9.6.

## 3. Scope
### 3.1 In Scope
- **S-2.12.1 Bank accounts (sample data).** The shop's bank accounts held as sample data for now (bank, branch, holder name, account number, opening balance), with three sample accounts available from the start, a fixed list of Sri Lankan banks to choose from, the ability to list accounts and add a new one, and rejection of a duplicate (same bank and account number). A completed sale remembers, for each payment, the account chosen for an Online payment, the last four card digits for a Card payment and the note for an Online payment, as they were at the time of sale.
- **S-2.12.2 Payment panel redesign.**
  - A grey summary box: number of items shown as "count / quantity", a divider, Sub Total (bold), Discount in rupees and Discount in percent (existing discount rules, gating and Admin approval unchanged).
  - A large bold Total row, the most prominent figure on the panel.
  - A payment-method card with four choices in one row: Cash (F3), Card (F4), Online (F7), Credit (no shortcut key). The selected one shows a grey fill with indigo text; each shows its shortcut key at the bottom-right.
  - Cash: "Cash Received" amount with a clear (X) button.
  - Card: "Paid" amount and an optional "Last 4 No." (exactly 4 digits if entered, no more than 4 can be typed), each with a clear (X) button.
  - Online: "Paid" amount with a clear (X) button; an "Account" selector with a small info icon and a "+" button to add a new account; an optional "Note" (up to 200 characters).
  - Credit: no amount entry; a read-only credit amount (Total minus the Cash, Card and Online amounts) and a message if Walk-in Customer is selected (a credit sale needs a named customer).
  - A grey Paid / Balance box with Paid and a larger Balance, keeping the existing "Change" / "Credit due" wording.
  - An actions area: Save & Print (F1, green, full width), then in a two-by-two grid Save (F2, purple outline), Hold (F10, amber outline), Save Quotation (F11, muted outline), New (F12, slate fill). Shortcut hints at the bottom-right of each button. Button behaviour is as before.
  - Split payment keeps working: switching method does not erase amounts already entered on another method.
  - The on-screen number keypad is removed from the billing screen.
  - Colour-coded buttons in light and dark themes, easy-to-hit controls, accessible names, and the project's contrast check extended to cover the new colours.
- **S-2.12.3 Create Account window.** The "+" button opens a "Create Account" window with required Bank (chosen from the fixed list), Branch, Holder Name, Account No and Opening Balance, red asterisks on required fields, a message beside any invalid field, Save (green) and Cancel (slate). On a successful Save the account is added, the window closes and the new account is selected for the Online payment.
- Adding the four reference screenshots to the design folder under the names listed in `docs/SOLUTION_TASKS.md` (see Dependencies).

### 3.2 Out of Scope (explicit)
- Any "More" payment method or menu; payment methods stay exactly Cash, Card, Online, Credit.
- The line-items table and the bill header.
- What Save Quotation does (unchanged; it remains the later-phase feature).
- Editing or deleting a bank account, a running account balance, account statements, posting Online payments to an account's balance, adding banks to the fixed list.
- Any real back-end or permanent storage; accounts and sales are sample data that are lost on reload like the rest of the app's sample data.
- A replacement for the removed on-screen keypad.
- A new shortcut key for Credit.
- Changes to how discounts, totals, holds, saves or receipts work beyond the layout described above.
- Pixel-for-pixel copying of the reference screenshots.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

**Summary and Total (S-2.12.2)**
- **AC-1** — Given a bill with items, when I view the payment panel, then a grey summary box shows No. of Items as "count / quantity", a divider, then Sub Total in bold, Discount (Rs.) and Discount (%).
- **AC-2** — Given the summary box, when I change the discount in rupees or percent, then it behaves exactly as before (linked values, maximum cap, setting-based gating and Admin approval for non-default discounts).
- **AC-3** — Given any bill, when I view the panel, then the Total is a large bold row with the label on the left and the amount on the right, and it is the most visually prominent figure on the panel.

**Payment methods (S-2.12.2)**
- **AC-4** — Given the payment panel, when I look at the method choices, then there are exactly four in one row, in the order Cash, Card, Online, Credit, and no "More" option exists.
- **AC-5** — Given the method row, when I look at each choice, then Cash shows F3, Card shows F4 and Online shows F7 at the bottom-right, Credit shows no shortcut key, and the selected choice has a grey fill with indigo text.
- **AC-6** — Given I press F3, F4 or F7 on the billing screen, when the key is handled, then the Cash, Card or Online choice is selected respectively.
- **AC-7** — Given Cash is selected, when I view the fields, then there is a "Cash Received" amount with a clear (X) button.
- **AC-8** — Given Card is selected, when I view the fields, then there is a "Paid" amount and a "Last 4 No." entry, each with a clear (X) button.
- **AC-9** — Given Card is selected, when I type in "Last 4 No.", then only digits are accepted and no more than 4 characters can be entered.
- **AC-10** — Given Card is selected and "Last 4 No." contains 1 to 3 digits, when I try to save, then saving is blocked with a message beside that field; given it is empty or has exactly 4 digits, then saving is not blocked by this field.
- **AC-11** — Given Online is selected, when I view the fields, then there is a "Paid" amount with a clear (X) button, an "Account" selector with an info icon and a "+" button beside it, and a "Note" box.
- **AC-12** — Given the Online "Note" box, when I type more than 200 characters, then the extra characters are not accepted.
- **AC-13** — Given Online is selected with a "Paid" amount above 0 and no account chosen, when I try to save, then saving is blocked with a message that names the Account field.
- **AC-14** — Given Online is selected with a "Paid" amount of 0 or empty, when I try to save, then a missing account does not block saving.
- **AC-15** — Given Credit is selected, when I view the fields, then there is no amount entry, and a read-only credit amount equals the Total minus the Cash, Card and Online amounts entered.
- **AC-16** — Given Credit is selected and Walk-in Customer is the chosen customer, when I view the panel, then a message says a named customer is needed for credit, and the sale cannot be saved with a credit amount above 0 until a named customer is chosen.
- **AC-17** — Given I have entered amounts on more than one method, when I switch between methods, then the amounts already entered on the other methods are kept, and Paid is the sum of all of them.

**Clear buttons (S-2.12.2)**
- **AC-18** — Given a clear (X) button next to an amount or "Last 4 No.", when I use it, then only that one field is emptied (an emptied amount counts as 0 in the totals) and keyboard focus returns to that field.
- **AC-19** — Given the clear buttons, when a screen reader reads them, then they are named "Clear Cash Received", "Clear Paid" and "Clear Last 4 No." as appropriate.

**Paid / Balance (S-2.12.2)**
- **AC-20** — Given amounts have been entered, when I view the grey Paid / Balance box, then it shows Paid (Rs.) and a larger Balance (Rs.), and the Balance label reads "Change" or "Credit due" exactly as before.

**Actions (S-2.12.2)**
- **AC-21** — Given the actions area, when I view it, then Save & Print (F1, green) is full width, followed by a two-by-two grid: Save (F2, purple outline) and Hold (F10, amber outline) on the first row; Save Quotation (F11, muted outline) and New (F12, slate fill) on the second; each shows its shortcut at the bottom-right.
- **AC-22** — Given any of these buttons or shortcut keys, when I use it, then it does exactly what it did before the redesign (save, print, hold, quotation, new bill).
- **AC-23** — Given the billing screen, when I look for the on-screen number keypad, then it is no longer shown anywhere and amounts are typed with the keyboard.

**Look and accessibility (S-2.12.2)**
- **AC-24** — Given the payment panel in light and in dark theme, when I view it, then the colour-coded buttons, grey boxes and active-choice styling are legible in both, and text meets 4.5:1 contrast and controls and borders meet 3:1; the project's contrast check covers the new colours and passes.
- **AC-25** — Given any button, choice or field on the panel, when I use a touch device, then it is at least 52px tall to hit, can be reached and used by keyboard alone, and has a clear accessible name; meaning never relies on colour alone (labels stay visible).
- **AC-26** — Given any screen other than the billing screen, when I view it, then it looks and behaves exactly as before.

**Bank accounts data (S-2.12.1)**
- **AC-27** — Given the app has just started, when I open the Online Account selector, then three sample accounts are listed: Bank of Ceylon (Colombo Fort), Commercial Bank of Ceylon (Nugegoda) and Sampath Bank (Maharagama), each held under "ShopDesk Pharmacy" with opening balance 0.00.
- **AC-28** — Given the Account selector, when I open it, then each option reads "Bank - Account No (Holder Name)" and options are sorted by bank.
- **AC-29** — Given the Bank field of Create Account, when I open it, then I can choose only from the fixed list of 24 banks in the PRD (A30), in the PRD's order, and cannot type a free-text bank or add a bank.
- **AC-30** — Given I save a sale with an Online payment, when the sale is stored, then it remembers the bank, account number and holder name of the account chosen at that time, and the Online note; given I later add other accounts, then the stored sale is unchanged.
- **AC-31** — Given I save a sale with a Card payment and a "Last 4 No." entered, when the sale is stored, then it remembers those four digits.
- **AC-32** — Given the sample data, when the app is reloaded, then accounts added in the previous session are gone and only the three sample accounts remain (consistent with other sample data).
- **AC-33** — Given an account exists, when I look for a way to edit or delete it, or to see a running balance or statement, then none is offered.

**Create Account window (S-2.12.3)**
- **AC-34** — Given the Online tab, when I choose the "+" button, then a "Create Account" window opens, keyboard focus is placed inside it and stays inside while it is open.
- **AC-35** — Given the window is open, when I press Esc, choose Cancel or choose the X icon, then it closes, nothing is added, and focus returns to the "+" button.
- **AC-36** — Given the window, when I view it, then Bank, Branch, Holder Name, Account No and Opening Balance each show a red asterisk to mark them required, Save is green and Cancel is slate.
- **AC-37** — Given I choose Save with required fields empty, when it validates, then a message appears beside each invalid field and nothing is added.
- **AC-38** — Given Branch or Holder Name, when I enter fewer than 2 or more than 60 characters, then a message appears beside that field.
- **AC-39** — Given Account No, when I type, then only digits are accepted; given fewer than 6 or more than 20 digits, then a message appears beside the field.
- **AC-40** — Given Opening Balance, when I enter a negative number, a non-number or more than two decimals, then a message appears beside the field; 0 and positive amounts with up to two decimals are accepted.
- **AC-41** — Given an existing account with the same Bank and Account No, when I try to save, then it is rejected with a message that the account already exists and the window stays open.
- **AC-42** — Given all fields are valid and not a duplicate, when I choose Save, then the account is added, the window closes, and the new account is already selected in the Online Account selector.
- **AC-43** — Given the window in light and dark themes, when I view it, then controls are at least 52px, every control can be used by keyboard alone, and contrast meets the same ratios as AC-24.

## 5. Security & Privacy Notes (business-level only)
- Sensitive data involved: the shop's bank account details (bank, branch, holder name, account number, opening balance) and the last four digits of a customer's card.
- Who should/shouldn't see it: bank account details are shop-internal and visible only to staff who can already use the billing screen (Admin, Manager, Cashier, Pharmacist). Only the last four card digits are ever kept; a full card number is never asked for or stored.
- No customer personal data is added. Account details are sample data in this phase; real account data must not be entered until a proper back end exists.
