## 1. Story
As a **pharmacy counter user (Admin, Manager, Cashier or Pharmacist)**, I want **a point-of-sale till reached from a "POS" button at the top of every page, where I can find products, build a bill, apply a discount only with an Admin's approval, take payment by Cash, Card, Online or Credit (including split payment), then save, print or hold the bill** so that **customers are served quickly at the counter, stock and today's sales stay accurate, and discounts are controlled by the owner without slowing down normal sales**.

## 2. Business Context
Until now ShopDesk covered the back office only. The pharmacy needs a till for counter sales (PRD section 5.9, goals G1 and G3). This is Phase A: a complete sell-and-take-payment till. It is frontend-only and works on sample data for the current session; there is no real payment, receipt-printer, drawer or barcode-scanner hardware. The written requirements are the specification and the ShopDesk look and feel governs styling. This is a single-pharmacy product: one selling price, no wholesale pricing, no transfers, no adding customers from the till. A sale reduces stock (earliest-expiry batch first) as a deliberate, confirmed exception to "only a goods-received note changes stock" (A11).

## 3. Scope
### 3.1 In Scope
- **S-2.9.1 POS button.** A blue "POS" button at the top of every back-office page, before the user's avatar, that opens the POS till; shows an active look while the till is open; usable by keyboard with a clear accessible name; not shown to users without POS access.
- **S-2.9.2 Sales records (sample data).** Sales, their lines, payments, held bills, customers (including a default "Walk-in Customer") and sales reps exist as sample data for the session. Saving a sale reduces stock earliest-expiry-first; bills can be held, resumed or discarded; each saved sale receives a unique invoice number; the Dashboard's Today's sales and Today's profit figures come from these saved sales.
- **S-2.9.3 Till screen.** The till opens as its own full-width screen reached only through the POS button. Its header shows the current date and time, a dark-mode toggle, a refresh control (asks for confirmation if the bill is unsaved) and one full-screen toggle button beside them, whose icon shows "enter full screen" or "exit full screen" to match the real state (including when the user leaves full screen with Esc or F11). The till never goes full screen by itself, only when the user clicks the toggle; the toggle is hidden if the browser cannot do full screen. The sidebar is hidden when the till opens if the Settings option "Hide sidebar when POS opens" is on, and can still be opened with the normal menu control. A "Back to back office" control returns to the Dashboard. Controls are large enough for touch (at least 52px) and the layout reflows on smaller screens.
- **S-2.9.4 Bill header.** Create New Bill and Hold Bill actions; bill date (today by default, editable); bill type Invoice only (no Transfer, no Retail/Wholesale choice); a generated invoice number; a customer chosen from the sample customer list (default Walk-in Customer, no adding a customer here); an optional sales rep.
- **S-2.9.5 Product search and bill lines.** Search by name, generic name, SKU or barcode (typing, or scan-style Enter) to add products. The bill lines show serial number, SKU, Rep, Barcode, Sale Price, Discount %, Quantity, Action (remove) and Total. Quantity and Discount % are editable per line (discount only when line discount is on in Settings). New lines start at the Settings default discount; any change from that default needs Admin approval (S-2.9.11); the discount can never exceed the Settings maximum. Stock-limit, out-of-stock and expired-product messages are shown.
- **S-2.9.6 Totals.** No. of Items, Sub Total, Discount (Rs.) and Discount (%) linked to each other, Total, Paid and Balance, all updating live. The bill discount fields are available only when bill discount is on in Settings; the bill discount starts at the Settings default, never exceeds the maximum or the Sub Total, and any change from the default needs Admin approval.
- **S-2.9.7 Payment.** Exactly four methods: Cash (F3), Card (F4), Online (F7) and Credit; no other method and no "More" option. Cash Received with change shown; split payment across methods; optional reference for Card and Online; Credit needs a named customer from the list and is blocked for Walk-in Customer with a message. Paid = Cash + Card + Online (Credit is not paid). Balance is shown with the words "Change" or "Credit due". An on-screen keypad (0-9, decimal point, Backspace) types into the amount field currently in use.
- **S-2.9.8 Bill actions and shortcuts.** Save & Print (F1, opens a printable receipt view in the browser), Save (F2), Hold (F10), New (F12). A list of held bills with resume and discard. Clear errors for an empty bill, an invalid payment, and stock that ran out between adding and saving. Shortcut keys work only on the till and each button shows its key.
- **S-2.9.9 POS access.** A "POS" row in the Users and roles permission matrix with Admin, Manager, Cashier and Pharmacist all Full (confirmed, A12). A user without access sees no POS button and a "no access" message if they open the till address directly. Discount approval is by Admin credentials for every role.
- **S-2.9.10 Settings "Point of sale" section.** Line discount on/off; bill discount on/off; one default discount % (used for both line and bill discounts, sample starting value 0%, not above the maximum); maximum discount % (a cap for everyone including Admin, sample starting value 20%); "Hide sidebar when POS opens" on/off (default on). Saved with Save changes, undone with Discard; the till reads these values.
- **S-2.9.11 Discount approval.** When anyone changes a line or bill discount to a value other than the default, a dialog asks for an Admin username and password. Only an active Admin account is accepted; success applies the discount and records the approver's name and time on the sale; wrong credentials, a non-Admin account or Cancel puts the discount back to the default with a message; the password is never kept or shown; going back to the default needs no approval; a value above the maximum is refused before the dialog appears; the dialog is keyboard operable with large controls.

### 3.2 Out of Scope (explicit)
- **Phase B (F-2.10):** returns, quotations (including F11), invoice and return lookup, the bottom-bar buttons (Last Invoice, Quotations, Cash Book, Update Products, Add OB, Counter Close, Bill & Reload, Drawer, Due Amount), cash book and counter close. In Phase A none of these buttons appear, not even disabled placeholders.
- Transfer bills, Retail/Wholesale pricing, adding a customer from the till, and the extra reference columns (Warranty, V.code, UOM, Code).
- Real payment or card-terminal integration, real receipt printer or cash drawer, real barcode-scanner hardware, and a real backend; all data lasts only for the current session.
- Credit limits, a customer module or customer dues collection.
- Sales rep commission effects (the rep is recorded only).
- Reload and Utility sales: handled separately and not part of the POS bill.
- Per-role discount approval levels and PIN prompts (withdrawn in PRD v0.7).

### 3.3 Unconfirmed assumptions (PRD OQ33, A18, A20 — built as stated unless the user says otherwise)
- **A20-1** Only an Admin account may approve a discount change; a Manager may not.
- **A20-2** A signed-in Admin still gets the approval dialog and must enter credentials.
- **A20-3** One default discount applies to both line and bill discounts; sample starting values are a default of 0% and a maximum of 20% (placeholders, adjustable in Settings).
- **A18** The "Hide sidebar when POS opens" setting stays (default on).
- One approval covers one change; changing the discount again asks again.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

### S-2.9.1 POS button
- **AC-1** — Given a signed-in user with POS access, when any back-office page is shown, then a blue "POS" button appears at the top of the page before the avatar.
- **AC-2** — Given the POS button, when the user clicks it, then the POS till opens.
- **AC-3** — Given the till is open, when the user looks at the POS button, then it shows an active look (not by colour alone).
- **AC-4** — Given the POS button, when the user tabs to it and presses Enter or Space, then the till opens, and a screen reader announces it as "POS".
- **AC-5** — Given a user whose role has no POS access, when pages are shown, then no POS button appears.
- **AC-6** — Given the dark and light themes, when the button is shown, then its label stays readable in both.

### S-2.9.2 Sales records and Dashboard
- **AC-7** — Given the till is first used in a session, when it opens, then sample customers (including "Walk-in Customer"), sample reps and the existing sample products and batches are available.
- **AC-8** — Given a bill with lines and valid payment, when it is saved, then a sale is recorded with its lines, payments, customer, rep, date, totals and any discount approval.
- **AC-9** — Given a saved sale, when stock is checked, then each product's stock is reduced by the sold quantity, taking from the batch expiring soonest first and moving to the next batch when one is used up.
- **AC-10** — Given two saved sales, when invoice numbers are compared, then each has a different, sequential invoice number.
- **AC-11** — Given a bill is held, when it is resumed, then the same lines, customer, discount and payments entries come back and the held entry is removed; when it is discarded, then it is removed and stock is untouched.
- **AC-12** — Given a held bill, when it sits on hold, then it does not reduce stock.
- **AC-13** — Given sales saved today, when the Dashboard is opened, then Today's sales equals the sum of those sales' totals and Today's profit reflects them.
- **AC-14** — Given no sales were saved today, when the Dashboard is opened, then Today's sales and Today's profit show zero rather than an error.

### S-2.9.3 Till screen
- **AC-15** — Given the till opens, when the screen loads, then it shows the date and time, a dark-mode toggle, a refresh control, a full-screen toggle and "Back to back office".
- **AC-16** — Given the till is not full screen, when the user clicks the full-screen toggle, then the browser enters full screen and the icon changes to "exit full screen".
- **AC-17** — Given the till is full screen, when the user clicks the toggle, presses Esc or presses F11, then full screen ends and the icon returns to "enter full screen".
- **AC-18** — Given the till opens, when it loads, then it does not enter full screen on its own.
- **AC-19** — Given a browser without full-screen support, when the till loads, then the toggle is not shown.
- **AC-20** — Given the toggle, when a screen reader reads it, then its name says the action it will do ("Enter full screen" or "Exit full screen").
- **AC-21** — Given "Hide sidebar when POS opens" is on, when the till opens, then the sidebar is hidden and the till uses the full width; when it is off, the sidebar is shown.
- **AC-22** — Given the sidebar is hidden, when the user uses the normal menu control, then the sidebar can be opened.
- **AC-23** — Given the till, when the user selects "Back to back office" and the bill has no unsaved content, then the Dashboard opens.
- **AC-24** — Given a bill with unsaved lines, when the user presses Refresh, New or leaves the till, then a confirmation is asked first and nothing is lost if the user chooses to stay.
- **AC-25** — Given a narrow screen, when the till is shown, then the layout reflows with no hidden controls and every button and key is at least 52px in size.

### S-2.9.4 Bill header
- **AC-26** — Given the till opens, when the bill header is shown, then the bill date is today, the bill type is Invoice, an invoice number is shown and the customer is "Walk-in Customer".
- **AC-27** — Given the bill type, when the user looks at the options, then only Invoice exists (no Transfer, no Retail/Wholesale choice).
- **AC-28** — Given the bill date, when the user changes it, then the new date is used on the saved sale.
- **AC-29** — Given the customer field, when the user opens it, then they can search and pick from the sample customers, and no way to add a customer exists there.
- **AC-30** — Given the rep field, when left empty, then the bill can still be saved; when a rep is picked, then it is recorded on the sale.
- **AC-31** — Given a bill in progress, when the user presses Create New Bill, then a fresh bill starts with a new invoice number (after a confirmation if the current bill is unsaved).
- **AC-32** — Given a bill with lines, when the user presses Hold Bill, then it is parked and a fresh bill opens.

### S-2.9.5 Product search and bill lines
- **AC-33** — Given products in stock, when the user types part of a name, generic name, SKU or barcode, then matching products are listed.
- **AC-34** — Given a result, when the user selects it, then a line is added to the bill with the product's selling price.
- **AC-35** — Given an exact barcode or SKU typed and Enter pressed, when it matches one product, then that product is added without further selection.
- **AC-36** — Given a product already on the bill, when it is added again, then its quantity goes up by one instead of adding a second line.
- **AC-37** — Given a product with zero stock, when it appears in results, then it is shown but cannot be added and a message says it is out of stock.
- **AC-38** — Given a product whose only stock is expired and Settings blocks expired sales, when the user tries to add it, then it is refused with a message saying it is expired.
- **AC-39** — Given the bill lines, when they are shown, then the columns are exactly S/N, SKU, Rep, Barcode, Sale Price, Discount %, QTY, Action and Total, with money right-aligned to two decimals.
- **AC-40** — Given a line, when the user edits quantity, then the line total and bill totals update immediately.
- **AC-41** — Given a quantity above the available stock, when the user enters it, then it is refused with a message naming the available amount.
- **AC-42** — Given a line, when the user selects remove, then it disappears and totals update.
- **AC-43** — Given line discount is off in Settings, when lines are shown, then the discount % cannot be edited.
- **AC-44** — Given line discount is on, when a line is added, then its discount % starts at the Settings default.
- **AC-45** — Given a line discount, when the user changes it to a different value within the maximum, then the approval dialog opens (see S-2.9.11) and the line keeps the new value only after approval.
- **AC-46** — Given a line discount, when the user enters a value above the maximum, then it is refused with a message and no dialog opens.
- **AC-47** — Given many lines on a small screen, when the table is wider than the screen, then it scrolls sideways and nothing is cut off.

### S-2.9.6 Totals
- **AC-48** — Given a bill, when lines change, then No. of Items, Sub Total, Discount, Total, Paid and Balance update immediately.
- **AC-49** — Given the bill discount in Rs., when the user changes it, then the Discount (%) updates to match, and the reverse.
- **AC-50** — Given bill discount is off in Settings, when the totals are shown, then the bill discount fields cannot be edited.
- **AC-51** — Given bill discount is on, when a new bill starts, then the bill discount starts at the Settings default.
- **AC-52** — Given a bill discount changed to a value other than the default, when the change is made (in Rs. or %), then the approval dialog opens, and the discount keeps the new value only after approval.
- **AC-53** — Given a bill discount above the maximum (as a percentage) or above the Sub Total, when entered, then it is refused with a message and no dialog opens.
- **AC-54** — Given an approved discount, when the user sets it back to the default, then no approval is asked.

### S-2.9.7 Payment
- **AC-55** — Given the payment area, when shown, then it offers exactly Cash, Card, Online and Credit, with no "More" option.
- **AC-56** — Given the shortcuts F3, F4 and F7, when pressed on the till, then Cash, Card and Online are selected respectively.
- **AC-57** — Given Cash Received higher than the amount due, when entered, then the Balance shows as "Change" with the amount to give back.
- **AC-58** — Given a Total of 100.00, when Cash 60.00 and Card 40.00 are entered, then the bill is fully paid with Paid 100.00 and no Credit due.
- **AC-59** — Given Card or Online is used, when the user enters an optional reference, then it is saved with the payment; leaving it empty is allowed.
- **AC-60** — Given a named customer and a Total of 100.00 with Cash 30.00, when Credit is chosen, then Credit due shows 70.00 and Paid shows 30.00.
- **AC-61** — Given the customer is Walk-in Customer, when the user chooses Credit, then it is blocked with a message asking for a named customer.
- **AC-62** — Given a bill that has a Credit amount, when the user changes the customer to Walk-in, then the bill cannot be saved until the Credit is removed or a named customer is chosen.
- **AC-63** — Given the on-screen keypad, when the user presses digits, the decimal point or Backspace, then the number appears in the amount field currently in use (Cash Received by default).
- **AC-64** — Given a second decimal point or more than two decimal places, when typed on the keypad, then it is ignored.
- **AC-65** — Given the keypad, when keys are shown, then each is at least 52px.
- **AC-66** — Given Balance, when shown, then it is labelled with the word "Change" or "Credit due" and not by colour alone.

### S-2.9.8 Bill actions and shortcuts
- **AC-67** — Given a valid bill, when the user presses Save (F2), then the sale is recorded, stock is reduced, a success message with the invoice number appears and a new empty bill opens.
- **AC-68** — Given a valid bill, when the user presses Save & Print (F1), then the sale is saved and a printable receipt view opens in the browser, showing items, discounts, total, payments and invoice number.
- **AC-69** — Given a bill with lines, when the user presses Hold (F10), then it is parked under a hold number and a new bill opens.
- **AC-70** — Given New (F12), when pressed with an unsaved bill, then a confirmation is asked first.
- **AC-71** — Given the held bills list, when opened, then each held bill shows its hold number, customer, item count, total and time, with Resume and Discard.
- **AC-72** — Given a bill in progress when Resume is chosen, then the user is asked to hold or discard the current bill (or it is held automatically) so nothing is lost.
- **AC-73** — Given an empty bill, when the user tries to Save or Hold, then it is refused with a message that the bill has no items.
- **AC-74** — Given payments totalling less than the Total with no Credit, when the user tries to save, then it is refused with a message that payment is incomplete.
- **AC-75** — Given Paid above the Total, when the extra is not cash, then saving is refused; when the extra is Cash, then it is allowed and shown as Change.
- **AC-76** — Given a product sold down by another bill since it was added, when the user saves, then saving is blocked with a message naming that product, and the bill stays on screen.
- **AC-77** — Given the user is on the till, when F1, F2, F3, F4, F7, F10 or F12 is pressed, then the matching action runs and the browser's own behaviour for that key does not interfere.
- **AC-78** — Given the user is on any other page, when those keys are pressed, then the till actions do not run.
- **AC-79** — Given the action buttons, when shown, then each displays its shortcut key as a visible hint.

### S-2.9.9 POS access
- **AC-80** — Given the Users and roles permission matrix, when opened, then it shows a "POS" row.
- **AC-81** — Given the default roles, when the POS row is read, then Admin, Manager, Cashier and Pharmacist are all Full.
- **AC-82** — Given a user whose POS access is removed, when they sign in, then no POS button is shown.
- **AC-83** — Given a user without POS access, when they open the till address directly, then a clear "you do not have access to POS" message is shown and the till does not load.
- **AC-84** — Given any signed-in role, when they change a discount away from the default, then Admin credentials are required regardless of their own role.
- **AC-85** — Given the existing modules, when POS access is added, then every other module's access is unchanged.

### S-2.9.10 Settings "Point of sale"
- **AC-86** — Given Settings, when opened by a user allowed to change them, then a "Point of sale" section shows line discount on/off, bill discount on/off, default discount %, maximum discount %, and "Hide sidebar when POS opens" on/off.
- **AC-87** — Given a fresh session, when the section is opened, then the default discount is 0%, the maximum is 20% and "Hide sidebar when POS opens" is on.
- **AC-88** — Given a default higher than the maximum, when the user tries to save, then it is refused with a message and nothing changes.
- **AC-89** — Given a percentage that is negative, above 100 or not a number, when the user tries to save, then it is refused with a message.
- **AC-90** — Given changes, when the user presses Save changes, then a confirmation appears and the next new bill in the till uses the new values.
- **AC-91** — Given changes, when the user presses Discard, then the previous saved values return.
- **AC-92** — Given a role not allowed to change Settings, when the section is opened, then the values are read-only or hidden consistent with existing Settings access.

### S-2.9.11 Discount approval
- **AC-93** — Given any user (including an Admin) changes a line or bill discount to a value other than the default and within the maximum, when the change is made, then a dialog opens asking for an Admin username and password.
- **AC-94** — Given the dialog, when it opens, then focus moves into it, keys cannot reach the page behind, Tab stays inside, Esc cancels, and its controls are at least 52px.
- **AC-95** — Given valid credentials of an active Admin account, when submitted, then the dialog closes and the new discount is applied.
- **AC-96** — Given an approved discount, when the sale is saved, then the approver's name and the time of approval are recorded on that sale.
- **AC-97** — Given a wrong username or password, when submitted, then the discount returns to the default and a message says the credentials were not accepted.
- **AC-98** — Given a valid account that is a Manager, Pharmacist or Cashier, or an inactive Admin, when submitted, then it is rejected with a message that an active Admin is required, and the discount returns to the default.
- **AC-99** — Given the dialog, when the user presses Cancel or Esc, then the discount returns to the default with a message that the change was not approved.
- **AC-100** — Given the password field, when typed or after the dialog closes, then the password is hidden while typing and is never shown or kept anywhere.
- **AC-101** — Given a discount equal to the default, when entered, then no dialog opens.
- **AC-102** — Given a discount above the maximum, when entered (even by an Admin), then it is refused with a message and no dialog opens.
- **AC-103** — Given an approved change, when the user changes the discount again to another non-default value, then approval is requested again.
- **AC-104** — Given a bill where the line discount and bill discount were each approved, when it is saved, then both approvals are recorded.

## 5. Security & Privacy Notes (business-level only)
- Sensitive data involved: Admin sign-in details typed into the approval dialog; customer names on sales; sales amounts and profit; the record of who approved a discount.
- Admin passwords must never be shown on screen or stored with the sale; only the approver's name and the time are kept.
- Discounts away from the default are controlled by an Admin even when a Manager or the Admin themself is signed in (assumed, see 3.3).
- Everyone with POS access can sell; only active Admin credentials can approve a discount change; the maximum discount cannot be exceeded by anyone.
- Staff without POS access must not see the POS button or be able to open the till.
- Profit figures on the Dashboard remain visible only to those who see them today.

Highlights: the Admin check is `AuthService.verifyCredentials` plus a re-read of the user (active, role ADMIN); the approval is a single-use token bound to scope, item and value; the password is read once from the dialog, cleared before the check and on every close path, and never stored; `canWrite(POS)` is re-checked in the services; save reads fresh products, re-validates, allocates FEFO and writes stock and sale in one synchronous chain; the maximum has no override; F-key handlers live only while the till component is alive.
- Decisions and deviations the reviewer and the user should know about:
    1. **AC-14 (Dashboard zero).** The Dashboard keeps its sample history, so Today's sales = sample figure + saved till sales for today. It shows zero only when there is neither a sample row nor a saved sale for today (no error either way). Saved sales are counted on the bill date.
    2. **AC-23 (Back to back office).** Goes to the Dashboard; a role without Dashboard access (the Cashier by default) goes to its first allowed page instead.
    3. **AC-24 (Refresh).** Refresh reloads products, settings and held bills and clears the bill (so it asks first when there are unsaved items). It does not reload the browser because that would wipe all session data. Leaving the till is guarded with a route guard, and the browser tab close or reload warns through `beforeunload` while there are unsaved items.
    4. **AC-72 (Resume).** The current bill is held automatically (the toast says under which hold number) so nothing is lost.
    5. **AC-31 (invoice numbers).** A number is reserved when a bill starts, so an abandoned bill leaves a gap; a till opened and closed without a bill gives its number back. Numbers always rise and are unique. The service re-checks uniqueness at save.
    6. **Discount approval at save.** The service verifies approvals structurally (matching scope, item and percent) because the sale store is a mock; a real backend must verify the approval itself.
    7. **No. of Items** counts bill lines; the **Rep** column shows the rep recorded on the bill (one rep per bill, AC-30).
    8. **Customer and Rep** use a searchable input with a list (type to search, pick from the list, anything else is refused); no add-customer control exists.
    9. **Bill date** cannot be in the future.
    10. **Bill discount** is stored either as a percentage or as an amount, whichever the cashier typed, so Rs. and % stay linked without rounding drift. If the lines change afterwards and an approved amount no longer matches its approved percentage, the screen warns and save refuses until it is set again.
    11. **Matrix order.** The POS row sits right after Reload & Utility.
    12. **Shared additions** (not in the Plan list, needed for the 52px rule and the receipt): Button `xl` size and Field `xl` size using a new `--sd-control-touch: 52px` token, `buttonSize` on the confirm dialog, `--sd-on-info` token for the blue POS pill (contrast pair added to `scripts/check-contrast.mjs`), global `styles/_print.scss` for the receipt (uses `:has()`), icons `maximize`, `minimize`, `refresh`, `pause`, `PermissionService.noteDenied` for the POS-specific no-access text.
    13. Stock is taken from the earliest-expiring batch first always (AC-9), whatever the "Sell the earliest-expiring batch first" Settings switch says, because the AC is unconditional.
