## 1. Story
As a **pharmacy counter user (Admin, Manager, Cashier or Pharmacist)**, I want **to take back returned goods and refund or credit the customer, save and later convert quotations, re-open the last sale, see and close the day's counter cash, collect payments on unpaid customer balances, quickly correct product details, and jump to Reload & Utility, all from the till** so that **everyday counter situations beyond a plain sale are handled in one place, stock and cash records stay correct, and the owner can trust the daily cash figures**.

## 2. Business Context
Phase A gave the pharmacy a till that sells and takes payment (story 2.9). Phase B adds the extras a counter needs around the sale: returns, quotations, reprinting, daily cash control and customer dues (PRD section 5.9, goals G1 and G3, requirements FR-POS-13 to FR-POS-15). Like Phase A it is frontend-only on sample data for the current session, with no cash-drawer, printer or payment hardware. It is a single-pharmacy product: no Transfer bill type, no "Normal" view button, no adding a customer from the till. Returns put stock back and quotations do not touch stock until converted, building on the confirmed rule that sales reduce stock earliest-expiry-first (A11). Phase B starts only after Phase A is accepted.

## 3. Scope
### 3.1 In Scope
- **S-2.10.1 Returns.** A second bill type, "Return", next to Invoice. The user finds the original sale by typing its invoice number, or finds an earlier return bill by its number. The original sale's lines are shown and the user selects which lines, and how many of each, are coming back (never more than was sold and not yet returned). Returned stock goes back into the batch it was sold from where possible, otherwise into the batch that sells first. The return ends with either a cash refund or a credit to a named customer, and a return record with its own number is kept. A printable return receipt is available. There is no Transfer bill type.
- **S-2.10.2 Quotations.** Save Quotation (F11) stores the current bill as a quotation without reducing stock and without counting as a sale. A Quotations list shows saved quotations; a quotation can be re-opened, converted to an invoice (which then follows normal Invoice rules and only then reduces stock) or deleted.
- **S-2.10.3 Last Invoice.** A "Last Invoice" button re-opens the most recently saved sale for viewing and re-printing. The Transfer bill type and the "Normal" view button do not exist.
- **S-2.10.4 Cash Book, Add OB and Drawer (End).** Cash Book shows the day's cash in and cash out from POS sales (including cash refunds on returns and cash taken on customer dues). Add OB records the opening balance of the till for the day. Drawer (End) ends the day's drawer session and shows the closing cash expected. Reload & Utility sales are handled separately and are excluded.
- **S-2.10.5 Counter Close.** The user enters the cash actually counted; the till shows expected cash, counted cash and the difference, then closes the counter and locks that day's counter report so it can no longer be changed. Expected cash counts POS sales only; Reload & Utility sales are excluded.
- **S-2.10.6 Due Amount, Update Products, Bill & Reload.** Due Amount lists customers who owe money (from Credit sales) with what each owes, and lets the user take a payment against a balance. Update Products is a quick way to correct a product's selling price and stock figure without leaving the till. Bill & Reload is a link that takes the user to Reload & Utility. No customer can be added from these screens.
- **S-2.10.7 Counter Close redesign (FR-POS-20).** The Counter Close screen is redesigned as a three-column, bilingual (English first, Sinhala beside it) screen that stacks on small screens: (1) a Shift Summary (Cashier, Register, Start Time, End Time, Total Sales) with a Sales Breakdown below it (Opening Amount, Cash In, Cash Out, Net Total); (2) a Cash Count card with an Expected Cash banner, the counted-cash field with a calculator helper for counting notes and coins, a Cash Handover to Supervisor field and a BF (Brought Forward) Balance for Next Shift field; (3) a Cash Reconciliation panel showing Expected, Counted and the Over, Short or Balanced result once a count is entered. The Complete Counter Close button stays disabled, with the hint "Enter cash count to enable completion", until a counted amount is entered. All S-2.10.5 rules stay: confirmation before closing, the day's report locked, the printable report; AC-51 to AC-60 still apply and AC-73 onward refine the layout and add handover and brought-forward. Visual reference: a screenshot supplied by the user that lives OUTSIDE the repository at `C:\Users\knd\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\d--Pharmacy-pharmacyShopDesk\625865e2-45ce-4551-a806-5f86cb4a4084\images\1.png`; per the user it is NOT to be copied into `docs/design/`. The reference also shows a Reload Commission line and button; those are deliberately NOT taken over (see Out of Scope).
- **Bottom bar.** The Phase B buttons (Last Invoice, Quotations, Cash Book, Update Products, Add OB, Counter Close, Bill & Reload, Drawer, Due Amount) appear on the till only now, and only for users with POS access.

### 3.2 Out of Scope (explicit)
- Transfer bills, the "Normal" view button, Retail/Wholesale pricing, and adding a customer from the till.
- Reload and Utility sales, which stay separate and are never part of the POS cash book, counter close, returns or dues.
- Real payment, refund-to-card, receipt printer, cash drawer or barcode-scanner hardware, and a real backend; all data lasts only for the current session.
- Credit limits, a full customer module, customer statements, or interest and reminders on dues.
- Sales rep commission effects.
- Returns of goods not sold through the till (no return without an original invoice), and supplier returns.
- Editing or deleting a saved sale, and re-opening a counter once it is closed and locked.
- Cost price, batch or expiry editing in the quick product update (those stay in Products and Stock in).
- Multiple tills or multiple counters (one counter per day).
- S-2.10.7: any Reload Commission line, amount or button on Counter Close (the reference screenshot shows them; OQ29 stays closed: Reload & Utility is excluded from the counter close and expected cash; Sales Breakdown is POS-only).
- S-2.10.7: multiple shifts or registers in a day; Sinhala wording for any label not visible in the reference screenshot (those stay English-only until the user or a native-speaker reviewer supplies text; no Sinhala is to be invented); Sinhala or bilingual labels anywhere other than this screen; a free arithmetic calculator.
- S-2.10.7: handover of cash being added to or taken off Expected Cash or the Over/Short result.

### 3.3 Unconfirmed assumptions (built as stated unless the Product Owner or user says otherwise)
- **B-1** Every role with POS access (Admin, Manager, Cashier, Pharmacist) may use all Phase B buttons; no extra approval is required for returns, counter close or product updates. (PRD FR-POS-12 gives all four roles full POS access and states no Phase B restriction.)
- **B-2** A return can only be made against an original invoice found by its number, and a customer credit from a return requires a named customer (Walk-in Customer gets a cash refund only).
- **B-3** Update Products edits the selling price and the stock figure, as the PRD says "quick stock/price edit". A stock change here is a deliberate exception to "only a goods-received note changes stock", like A11, so it asks for confirmation and shows the old and new values.
- **B-4** One counter per day: one opening balance, one counter close, and the closed day is locked.
- **B-5** Taking a payment on a customer's due reduces the balance owed and counts as cash in when paid in cash.

**Confirmed decisions for S-2.10.7 (user confirmed all five defaults, OQ36 resolved; PRD assumptions A22 to A26):**
- **A22** Cash handed over to a named supervisor (an Admin or Manager) is saved and printed on the report; it does not change Expected Cash or the Over/Short result, which compare Expected with the full count.
- **A23** Brought Forward = counted cash minus handover; it is saved in the report and becomes the next business day's opening balance.
- **A24** One counter close per business day. Register is the single till, shown as "Counter 1". Start Time is the first POS sale or opening-balance time of the day; End Time is the moment the close is completed.
- **A25** The calculator button opens a denomination counter for Sri Lankan rupee notes and coins that fills the counted amount; it is keyboard operable and accessible.
- **A26** Sinhala labels apply to this screen only; only the Sinhala text visible in the reference screenshot is used.
- Note for the Team Lead: the PRD's Sales Breakdown lists Opening Amount, Cash In, Cash Out and Net Total (POS only), which keeps Expected Cash consistent with AC-52; the brief's shorthand "Opening Amount plus POS sales" is read the same way.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

### S-2.10.1 Returns
- **AC-1** — Given the bill type choice, when the user opens it, then it offers Invoice and Return, and no Transfer.
- **AC-2** — Given Return is chosen, when the bill header is shown, then an invoice-number lookup and a return-bill lookup are available.
- **AC-3** — Given an invoice number that exists, when the user looks it up, then the original sale's customer, date and lines are shown.
- **AC-4** — Given an invoice number that does not exist, when looked up, then a clear "no sale found" message is shown and nothing is added to the return.
- **AC-5** — Given an earlier return bill number, when looked up, then that return is shown for viewing and re-printing, and it cannot be returned a second time.
- **AC-6** — Given the original sale's lines, when the user selects a line and a quantity, then that line is added to the return with the price actually charged on the sale (including any discount).
- **AC-7** — Given a line that was sold in a quantity of 5 and 2 were already returned, when the user tries to return 4, then it is refused with a message that only 3 remain returnable.
- **AC-8** — Given a return with at least one line, when the user completes it, then each returned quantity is added back to stock, into the batch it was originally sold from where that batch still exists, otherwise into the batch that sells first.
- **AC-9** — Given a return, when it is completed as a refund, then the cash to hand back equals the returned value and appears as cash out on the Cash Book.
- **AC-10** — Given a return against a named customer, when the user chooses customer credit, then the returned value is recorded as credit for that customer and reduces what they owe if they have a balance.
- **AC-11** — Given a return against Walk-in Customer, when the user looks at the options, then only a cash refund is offered and credit is not.
- **AC-12** — Given a completed return, when the record is checked, then it has its own unique return number, the original invoice number, the lines, the refund or credit chosen, the date and time and who processed it.
- **AC-13** — Given a completed return, when the user chooses to print, then a printable return receipt opens showing the returned items, amount, refund or credit and numbers.
- **AC-14** — Given a return with no lines selected, when the user tries to complete it, then it is refused with a message that nothing is selected.
- **AC-15** — Given a return in progress, when the user leaves the return or starts a new bill, then a confirmation is asked first and nothing is lost if the user stays.
- **AC-16** — Given a completed return, when the Dashboard is opened, then Today's sales reflects the returned value being taken off.
- **AC-17** — Given a sale that included Reload or Utility items sold elsewhere, when a return is made, then only the till sale is affected.

### S-2.10.2 Quotations
- **AC-18** — Given a bill with lines, when the user presses Save Quotation (F11), then a quotation is stored with its own quotation number and the bill clears for a new one.
- **AC-19** — Given a saved quotation, when stock and Today's sales are checked, then neither has changed.
- **AC-20** — Given an empty bill, when the user presses Save Quotation, then it is refused with a message that the bill has no items.
- **AC-21** — Given the F11 key, when pressed on the till, then Save Quotation runs and the browser's own behaviour for that key does not interfere; on any other page it does nothing; the button shows its key.
- **AC-22** — Given saved quotations, when the user opens Quotations, then each shows its number, customer, date, item count, total and status, newest first.
- **AC-23** — Given a quotation, when the user opens it, then its lines, discounts and customer are shown as saved.
- **AC-24** — Given a quotation, when the user chooses Convert to invoice, then it loads onto the till as an Invoice bill ready for payment.
- **AC-25** — Given a converted quotation, when the invoice is saved, then stock is reduced earliest-expiry-first at that moment, and the quotation is marked converted and shows the invoice number.
- **AC-26** — Given a quotation whose product has since sold out or expired, when it is converted, then the affected lines are flagged with the same stock and expiry messages as a normal bill, and saving is blocked until fixed.
- **AC-27** — Given a quotation whose prices have changed since it was saved, when it is converted, then current selling prices apply and the user is told prices were updated.
- **AC-28** — Given a quotation that is converted or deleted, when the list is checked, then a converted one cannot be converted again and a deleted one no longer appears.
- **AC-29** — Given a quotation with a discount away from the default, when it is converted, then the discount rules of Phase A still apply: the approval is asked again for any non-default discount.

- **AC-56** — Given the user confirms, when the counter is closed, then a counter report is saved for that day showing opening balance, cash in, cash out, expected, counted, difference, who closed it and when.
- **AC-57** — Given a closed counter, when anyone tries to change that day's report, the opening balance or its cash entries, then it is refused with a message that the day is locked.
- **AC-58** — Given a closed counter, when the user opens Counter Close again for the same day, then the locked report is shown for viewing and printing.
- **AC-59** — Given the counter is closed for today, when a new sale is saved, then the user is warned that today's counter is closed and told the sale will not be part of the locked report.

## 5. Security & Privacy Notes (business-level only)
- Sensitive data involved: customer names and the amounts they owe; cash opening, closing, counted and difference figures; refund amounts; who processed a return or closed a counter and when; sales amounts.
- Cash figures and counter reports should be visible only to staff who have POS access; staff without POS access must not see the bottom-bar buttons or reach these screens.
- A closed day's counter report must not be changeable by anyone, including an Admin, so the owner can trust it.
- S-2.10.7 adds cash handover and brought-forward figures and the name of the receiving supervisor to the sensitive data; they are visible only to staff with POS access and are part of the locked report.
- Every return, payment on a due, opening balance change, product price or stock change and counter close records who did it and when.
- No card, bank or payment-device details are captured; Card and Online payments keep only the method and an optional reference.
