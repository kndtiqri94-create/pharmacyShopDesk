- **Workstream:** 2 — E-2 ShopDesk back office frontend, Feature F-2.5 Services and settings (stories S-2.5.1 and S-2.5.2)

## 1. Story
As a **pharmacy owner, manager, pharmacist or cashier**, I want **to sell mobile reloads and take bill payments at the counter while seeing how much reload float I have left and being warned when it runs low, and, as the administrator, to set how the shop runs (shop details, region, stock alert rules and backup choices) in one place** so that **the counter can serve customers quickly without running out of float unexpectedly, every transaction stays listed for follow-up, and the shop's own alert rules decide what the rest of ShopDesk flags as low, expiring or needing a top up**.

## 2. Business Context
Reload & Utility is the counter-service side of the shop: customers buy mobile credit and pay utility bills, and the shop earns a commission on each reload from a limited balance (the float) that must be topped up. Settings is the one place where the shop's own rules are kept; three of its values already drive other screens using default sample values from F-2.1 (the expiry alert window used by Products and the Dashboard, the default reorder level used by Products, and the low-float level used by the Dashboard "Needs attention" list), so this feature lets the administrator change them and see the effect everywhere. It serves PRD goals G1 and G3 and builds on F-2.1 (look and feel, building blocks, sample data, main frame, role access), F-2.2 (Dashboard and Products), and F-2.4 (role permissions that can be edited). Colour values remain approximate until exact ones are supplied (PRD OQ1).

## 3. Scope
### 3.1 In Scope
- **S-2.5.1 Reload & Utility.**
  - Heading "Reload & utility" with the subtitle "Sell mobile reloads and take bill payments at the counter." and the buttons Statement and a primary Top up float.
  - A "Mobile reload" card: four operator tiles (Dialog, Mobitel, Hutch, Airtel) of which one can be selected; Mobile number (required, with a +94 prefix); Customer name (optional); Amount chips Rs. 50, 100, 200, 500, 1,000 and Other (which lets a custom amount be typed); a live commission preview showing "Commission you earn" and "Customer pays"; and the buttons Clear and a primary Send reload.
  - A "Reload float" card: Available float in rupees, a progress bar, a warning when the float is below the low-float level, and today's figures for Reloads today, Bills paid today and Commission today.
  - A "Bill payment" card: Provider, Account number, Amount (Rs.), the hint about the service fee added at the counter, and a Pay bill button.
  - A "Recent transactions" table (Time, Provider with type, Number or account, Amount (Rs.), Commission, Status of Success, Pending or Failed) with an Export button. Failed and Pending rows stay listed so they can be retried or refunded later.
  - Send reload lowers the float; Top up float raises it; the card figures, the Dashboard "Needs attention" low-float row and the transactions list always agree.
- **S-2.5.2 Settings.**
  - Heading "Settings" with the subtitle "One shop, one place to change how it runs." and the buttons Discard and a primary Save changes.
  - A left list of setting groups and a right card showing the chosen group's section: Shop profile, Currency & region, Inventory alerts and Backup & sync.
  - Shop profile: Shop name (required), Registration no, Address, Phone, Email, and Shop logo with an Upload logo button and the hint "PNG or JPG, at least 200 px. Shown on printed documents."
  - Currency & region: Currency, Date format and Time zone, shown with the shop's current values (Lankan rupee, 21 Sep 2026 style, Asia/Colombo).
  - Inventory alerts: Warn about expiry (days), Default reorder level (units), the low-float level (Rs.), and the options "Sell the earliest-expiring batch first (FEFO)", "Block sales of expired batches" and "Show low-stock count in the sidebar".
  - Backup & sync: a status notice (for example "Everything is backed up. Last sync today 09:12 · next automatic sync in 5 minutes."), the instant option "Sync automatically when online", and the buttons Back up now and Sync now.
  - Changes apply only when Save changes is pressed, with Discard to revert, except the Backup & sync options, which apply the moment they are changed.
  - Saved values for the expiry window, default reorder level, low-float level and sidebar low-stock count take effect across the app straight away.
- Accepted assumptions (consistent with F-2.1 to F-2.4 and PRD open questions; none changes a confirmed requirement):
  - OQ4: Statement, Export (transactions), Upload logo, Back up now and Sync now are shown but do nothing real and give a clear "sample only" message. Retry and refund of Failed and Pending transactions are not provided; those rows simply stay listed.
  - All data lives only for the current session, as in earlier features; refreshing returns to the original sample set, including sent reloads, bill payments, float changes and saved settings.
  - Commission is 2.5% of the reload amount for every operator in the sample, shown to two decimals (for example Rs. 500 gives Rs. 12.50 commission; the customer pays Rs. 500.00). With no amount chosen, the preview shows Rs. 0.00 for both.
  - A mobile number is the nine digits that follow the +94 prefix (for example 77 123 4567); spaces typed are ignored and the number must be digits only.
  - Send reload needs an operator, a valid mobile number and an amount above zero that is not more than the Available float. Customer name is optional.
  - Send reload lowers the Available float by the reload amount (not by amount minus commission). It adds one to Reloads today and adds the commission to Commission today, and adds a Success row at the top of Recent transactions.
  - Top up float asks for an amount above zero (whole rupees, no more than Rs. 1,000,000); confirming raises the float by that amount, shows a short confirmation and records nothing in Recent transactions.
  - The progress bar shows the Available float against a full float of Rs. 20,000 in the sample (shown as full at or above that level).
  - The low-float warning shows when the Available float is below the low-float level from Settings (Rs. 10,000 by default) and uses the wording "Float is below Rs. 10,000. Top up before the evening rush." with the current level in place of 10,000.
  - Pay bill needs a provider, an account number and an amount above zero. A fixed service fee of Rs. 25.00 is added at the counter, so the customer pays the amount plus Rs. 25.00. Paying a bill adds one to Bills paid today, adds a Success row to Recent transactions, and does not change the reload float or the commission.
  - Provider choices in the sample are Electricity (CEB), Water (NWSDB), Telephone (SLT) and Internet. Reload rows show the operator and "Reload"; bill rows show the provider and "Bill payment".
  - Recent transactions are listed newest first. Pagination appears only at more than 10 rows.
  - The sample transactions contain at least one Success, one Pending and one Failed row.
  - Settings groups: the left list offers the four sections named in the PRD (Shop profile, Currency & region, Inventory alerts, Backup & sync). The extra design groups "Receipts & printing" and "Devices" are not built in this phase. Choosing a group scrolls to or shows that group's section.
  - Currency, Date format and Time zone are fixed display values in this phase and cannot be changed.
  - Valid values: Shop name must not be empty; Email, when given, must look like an email address; Expiry warning is a whole number of days from 1 to 365; Default reorder level is a whole number of units from 1 to 10,000; the low-float level is a whole-rupee amount from 0 to 1,000,000.
  - The low-float level has a field in Inventory alerts, labelled "Warn when reload float is below (Rs.)". The design places the threshold under settings without showing a field for it; this is the assumed place (see Open Items).
  - Save changes and Discard are unavailable while nothing has been changed. Leaving the Settings page without saving discards the unsaved edits.
  - The default reorder level pre-fills the Reorder level when adding a new product; products that already have a reorder level are not changed. A saved expiry window changes which batches count as Expiring soon, and therefore the Products status chips and counts, the Dashboard Expiring soon tile and its note, and the Dashboard "Needs attention" row.
  - "Show low-stock count in the sidebar" controls whether the count beside Products in the sidebar is shown; switching it off hides that count.
  - FEFO and "Block sales of expired batches" are saved and shown as set; because selling happens outside this back office in this phase, they have no further visible effect.
  - The Backup & sync automatic-sync option applies instantly with a short confirmation and does not wait for Save changes. Back up now and Sync now update nothing real; the status notice stays as shown.
- Role rules from the permission matrix apply: Reload & Utility is Full for Admin, Manager and Cashier, and View for Pharmacist; Settings is Full for Admin and No access for Manager, Pharmacist and Cashier. Because Admin can edit roles in Users & roles (F-2.4), the current permissions in the session always decide.

### 3.2 Out of Scope (explicit)
- Real reload or bill-payment gateways, real operator or utility lookups, receipts or printed slips (PRD NG5).
- Retrying, refunding or cancelling Failed or Pending transactions (they only stay listed), and editing or deleting past transactions.
- Real statements and real export files (Statement and Export are sample only).
- Per-operator commission rates, commission settings, and changing the service fee.
- Real file upload for the logo, real backup or sync, and real storage of any setting beyond the current session.
- The Receipts & printing and Devices groups, tax settings, and changing currency, date format or time zone.
- Real selling behaviour for FEFO and blocking expired batches (the point of sale is outside this back office).
- Users, roles and permission editing (F-2.4) and the content of earlier modules other than the effects of saved settings listed above.
- Exact colour values (OQ1), numeric performance targets (OQ6) and the smallest supported width (OQ5).
- Languages other than English; multi-branch features.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)
Cross-cutting rules for every screen below (not repeated per criterion): works in light and dark themes, reflows on smaller screens, follows the design's look and content rules (plain language, `Rs. 84,250`, `21 Sep 2026`, money columns headed "(Rs.)" with two decimals and right-aligned, no emoji or exclamation marks), is operable by keyboard with visible focus, status always shown as a word, only one primary button per screen, and respects role access. Till-style controls (operator tiles, Send reload) are large enough to tap easily.

## 5. Security & Privacy Notes (business-level only)
- Sensitive data involved: customers' mobile numbers and names, utility account numbers and amounts paid, how much float and commission the shop holds, and the shop's own details (name, registration number, address, phone, email, logo). Settings values decide what the shop is warned about. All sample data is fictional.
- Who should/shouldn't be able to see or change this: only signed-in people see anything. Cashiers and Managers can run the reload counter but must not see or change Settings. Pharmacists may read Reload & Utility but must not send reloads, pay bills or top up the float, and must not see Settings. Only Admin may change Settings. A person without the required access must not be able to reach a screen or action by typing its address. Customer mobile numbers and account numbers are shown only in the transactions list to people with access to Reload & Utility. A Settings change must not be lost silently and an unsaved change must never apply by accident.
