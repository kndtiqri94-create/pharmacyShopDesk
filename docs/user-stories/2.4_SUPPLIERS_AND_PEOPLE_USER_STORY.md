## 1. Story
As a **pharmacy owner, manager or administrator**, I want **to see my suppliers and what I owe each of them and record payments against their deliveries, to see my staff with their attendance and leave, and to control who can sign in to ShopDesk and what each role can do** so that **I always know what is owed to whom, who is working and who can log in, and each person only sees and changes what their job needs, without losing the history of anyone who leaves**.

## 2. Business Context
Suppliers and people complete the "inventory" and "people" sides of the back office. F-2.3 Stock-in already records each delivery with what was paid at the time; this feature lets the shop settle the balance later, oldest invoice first, and keeps supplier balances, delivery payment statuses and the Dashboard-level figures consistent. Employees are the staff records; Users are the sign-ins, and a sign-in belongs to at most one employee. The role permission table becomes the single source of who can open what. It serves PRD goals G1 and G3 and builds on F-2.1 (look and feel, building blocks, sample data, main frame, role access), F-2.2 (products) and F-2.3 (GRNs and purchase orders). Colour values remain approximate until exact ones are supplied (PRD OQ1).

## 3. Scope
### 3.1 In Scope
- **S-2.4.1 Suppliers.**
  - Heading "Suppliers" with a subtitle giving the number of active suppliers and the total outstanding (for example "5 active suppliers · Rs. 142,600 outstanding") and a primary Add supplier button.
  - A list card with search (supplier or contact), chips All / Owing / Inactive with live counts, and a table of Supplier (with initials avatar and contact name), Owing (Rs.), Last order and Status.
  - A detail panel beside the list for the selected supplier: initials avatar, name, "Supplier since <year>", status badge, Outstanding amount with a Record payment button, contact person, phone, email, payment terms and delivery time (for example "30 days credit · delivers in 2 days"), a Recent activity list (for example a purchase order sent or overdue, a delivery received and unpaid, a payment made), and Edit and New PO buttons.
  - Record payment: settles that supplier's received GRNs oldest first, reducing Outstanding and updating each affected GRN to Paid, Part paid or Unpaid.
  - Inactive suppliers stay in the list for history and are hidden wherever a supplier is chosen (New GRN and New purchase order).
- **S-2.4.2 Employees.**
  - Heading "Employees" with a subtitle giving the number of staff and how many are on shift today (for example "8 staff · 5 on shift today") and the buttons Export and a primary Add employee.
  - Four summary tiles: Total staff, On shift today, On leave, Can log in.
  - Three tabs: Staff (with count), Attendance, Leave (with count).
  - On the Staff tab: search (name, ID or phone), chips All / Active / On leave with live counts, and a table of Employee (avatar, name and ID such as EMP-001), Role, Phone, Shift, System login (the role, or "No login") and Status, with row actions.
  - A "Today's attendance" card listing each person as Present, Late, Not yet or On leave (with the time in where relevant, for example "In 07:52") and a Mark attendance button.
- **S-2.4.3 Users and roles.**
  - Heading "Users & roles" with the subtitle "Who can sign in to ShopDesk and what they can do." and a primary Add user button.
  - A Users card showing the number of active users and, for each user, their name with username and employee ID (or "no employee"), role badge, last sign-in, status (Active or Disabled), and edit and lock actions.
  - A Role permissions card with an Edit roles button and a table of every module against the four roles (Admin, Manager, Pharmacist, Cashier) showing Full, View or No access.
  - An Add user panel: Employee (required), Username or email (required), Role (required, with the hint "Cashiers can sell and take reloads, nothing else."), Temporary password (required), Quick PIN for the till (hint "Used to unlock the POS screen."), the option "Change password at first sign-in", and Cancel and Create user buttons.
  - Disabling a user stops them signing in but keeps all their history.
  - Changing the permission table changes what the signed-in session can open, within the sample.
- Accepted assumptions (consistent with F-2.1, F-2.2, F-2.3 and PRD open questions; none changes a confirmed requirement):
  - OQ4: Export, Add supplier, Edit (supplier), Add employee, Mark attendance and the employee row actions are shown but do nothing real and give a clear "sample only" message. Quick PIN is accepted and stored for the session only, with no effect on sign-in.
  - All data lives only for the current session, as in earlier features; refreshing returns to the original sample set.
  - Outstanding for a supplier is the total balance still owed on that supplier's received GRNs (as already defined for "Unpaid to suppliers" in F-2.3). A supplier is "Owing" when that amount is above zero.
  - Record payment asks for an amount, pre-filled with the full Outstanding; the amount must be above zero and no more than Outstanding. The payment is applied to the oldest received unpaid GRN first (by received date, then GRN number), fully settling each in turn; the last one touched may be left part paid.
  - After a payment, a GRN's payment status follows the F-2.3 rule: Paid when nothing is left to pay, Part paid when something has been paid and something remains, Unpaid when nothing has been paid.
  - New PO on a supplier opens the New purchase order form with that supplier already chosen.
  - Recent activity shows the supplier's latest five events, newest first, drawn from their purchase orders, GRNs and payments.
  - A supplier's status is Active or Inactive from the sample data; there is no screen in this feature to change it (Add supplier and Edit are sample only).
  - The Attendance tab shows every employee's attendance for today in a table (employee, status, time in); the Leave tab shows the employees currently on leave with their return date. Both are read-only, drawn from the sample data.
  - On shift today counts people who are Present or Late today; Late people are also counted as on shift.
  - Users are linked to at most one employee, and an employee who already has a sign-in is not offered when adding a user. Username or email must be unique among users.
  - The temporary password must be at least 8 characters and the Quick PIN, when given, must be exactly 4 digits.
  - The edit action on a user lets the role be changed in the side panel; the employee link and username stay as they are. The lock action toggles a user between Active and Disabled, with a plain confirmation before disabling.
  - The signed-in person cannot disable their own sign-in. The Admin role is fixed at Full on everything in the Edit roles view, so the shop can never lock itself out.
  - Edit roles lets Admin change any non-Admin role's access for any module to Full, View or No access, applied when the changes are saved; Discard returns to the saved matrix.
  - Newly created users sign in with the temporary password in the sample session (mock users).
- Role rules from the permission matrix apply: Suppliers is Full for Admin and Manager, View for Pharmacist, and No access for Cashier. Employees is Full for Admin, View for Manager, and No access for Pharmacist and Cashier. Users is Full for Admin and No access for Manager, Pharmacist and Cashier.

### 3.2 Out of Scope (explicit)
- Creating or editing a supplier's details, changing a supplier's active or inactive status, and creating or editing employee records (the buttons are shown as sample only).
- Marking attendance for real, approving or requesting leave, rosters and payroll.
- Real export files, real till PIN unlocking, real password rules beyond those stated, password reset, "Forgot password?" and any real authentication or storage.
- Payment methods, receipts or cheque details for a supplier payment; supplier statements; returns to suppliers and credit notes.
- Creating new GRNs or purchase orders from this feature other than opening New purchase order for the chosen supplier.
- Deleting users or employees. Disabling is the only way to remove a sign-in.
- Settings screens (F-2.5) and Reload & Utility.
- Exact colour values (OQ1), numeric performance targets (OQ6) and the smallest supported width (OQ5).
- Languages other than English; multi-branch features.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)
Cross-cutting rules for every screen below (not repeated per criterion): works in light and dark themes, reflows on smaller screens, follows the design's look and content rules (plain language, `Rs. 84,250`, `21 Sep 2026`, money columns headed "(Rs.)" with two decimals and right-aligned, no emoji or exclamation marks), is operable by keyboard with visible focus, status always shown as a word, only one primary button per screen, and respects role access.

### 4.1 Suppliers: overview, list and filtering (S-2.4.1)
- **AC-1** — Given I open Suppliers, when the page loads, then I see the heading "Suppliers", a subtitle stating the number of active suppliers and the total outstanding that agrees with the sum of the Owing column, and a primary Add supplier button.
- **AC-2** — Given I choose Add supplier, when it is pressed, then a clear "sample only" message appears and nothing else changes.
- **AC-3** — Given the list card, when I type in the search box, then the list narrows to suppliers whose name or contact person contains what I typed, ignoring upper/lower case, and the chip counts update.
- **AC-4** — Given the chips All, Owing and Inactive, when I view them, then each shows a live count matching its suppliers (Owing means something is still owed to them), exactly one is selected at a time, and choosing one shows only those suppliers.
- **AC-5** — Given the table, when I read a row, then I see the supplier name with an initials avatar and the contact person underneath, the amount owing in rupees (right-aligned, two decimals), the date of the last order, and a status badge (Active or Inactive). Given nothing matches the search or filter, then a plain message says no suppliers match and how to clear the filters. Given 10 or fewer rows, then no pagination appears.
- **AC-6** — Given the list is shown, when the page first opens, then one supplier is selected and its detail panel is shown; given I choose another row, then the panel changes to that supplier and the chosen row is visibly selected.

### 4.2 Suppliers: detail panel (S-2.4.1)

- **AC-8** — Given a selected supplier, when I read Recent activity, then I see up to five of their latest events, newest first, each saying what happened and its state, for example a purchase order that is sent and overdue, a GRN that is received and unpaid, or a payment that is paid.
- **AC-9** — Given I choose Edit, when it is pressed, then a clear "sample only" message appears; given I choose New PO, then the New purchase order form opens with this supplier already chosen and its side card showing the same amount owed as in this panel.
- **AC-10** — Given a supplier is Inactive, when I choose a supplier in New GRN or New purchase order, then that supplier is not offered; the supplier remains visible here with its history and its status shown as Inactive. Given a supplier owes nothing, then Outstanding shows Rs. 0.00 and Record payment is unavailable.

### 4.3 Supplier payments (S-2.4.1)
- **AC-11** — Given a supplier with an outstanding amount, when I choose Record payment, then I am asked for the amount, pre-filled with the full Outstanding, with a way to confirm and a way to cancel.

- **AC-13** — Given a valid payment, when I confirm, then the supplier's Outstanding goes down by exactly that amount, a short confirmation is shown, and the panel and list both show the new figure straight away.
- **AC-14** — Given a supplier owes on several received GRNs, when I record a payment, then it is applied to the oldest unpaid received GRN first, fully settling it before moving to the next; given the amount runs out part-way through a GRN, then that GRN is left part paid with the correct balance.
- **AC-15** — Given a payment settles GRNs, when I open the GRN list, then each affected GRN shows its new payment status (Paid, Part paid or Unpaid) and the GRN totals tile "Unpaid to suppliers" has gone down by the amount paid, with its invoice count and oldest-age note updated.
- **AC-16** — Given I record a payment, when I read Recent activity, then a new Payment entry appears at the top; given I cancel the payment, then nothing changes.
- **AC-17** — Given a payment has been recorded, when I look at the New purchase order side card for that supplier, the Suppliers subtitle and any other place the amount owed is shown, then they all show the same figure.

## 5. Security & Privacy Notes (business-level only)
- Sensitive data involved: supplier contact details (names, phone numbers, email addresses) and what the shop owes each supplier; employee personal details (names, phone numbers, shifts, attendance and leave); the list of who can sign in and with what role, last sign-in times; temporary passwords and quick PINs; and the table that decides who can see what. Supplier balances are commercially sensitive; staff details are personal data. All sample data is fictional.
- Who should/shouldn't be able to see or change this: only signed-in people see anything. Cashiers must not see supplier balances, staff details or user accounts. Pharmacists may read suppliers but not change them or record payments, and must not see staff details or user accounts. Managers may manage suppliers and payments, may read but not change staff records, and must not see user accounts or the permission table. Only Admin may create, change or disable users and change what each role can do. A person without the required access must not be able to reach a screen or action by typing its address. Temporary passwords and quick PINs must never be shown back after they are entered. Nobody can disable their own sign-in, and the Admin role cannot lose its access, so the shop cannot lock itself out. Disabling a user must never remove or rewrite the history of what they did.
